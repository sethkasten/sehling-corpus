# The chapter guides (archived)

These seventeen guides were written one at a time and then assembled, each as one chapter, into
*Liturgical and Ecclesiastical Life in Reformation Germany*,
[`LITURGICAL_AND_ECCLESIASTICAL_LIFE.md`](../LITURGICAL_AND_ECCLESIASTICAL_LIFE.md). They are kept
here as they stood when the book was assembled, and they are no longer maintained. The book is
the current text: corrections made since are in the book only. The book also numbers each
section with its chapter first, so that §3 of the Church Orders guide is §1.3 of the book.

Their links to one another still work, and so do their links to the guides kept at the top of
the repository.
