# hymnal-project

## Files

**KELG-Hymn-Index.xlsx** has all the hymns from KELG (Kirchen-Gesangbuch für Evangelisch-Lutherische Gemeinden, i.e. Walther's Hymnal in the original German) along with the hymn number and the English counterparts in ELHB, TLH, LW, ELH and LSB (not Worship Supplement 1969 or Hymnal Supplement 1998). Its last column has each hymn's English first line in Walther's Hymnal (WH), the English translation of KELG; the 1917 supplement (KELG 444–485) isn't in WH, so those have none (see kelg_english.csv for translations found elsewhere). In hymnal.db this is the `kelg_index` view, with the hymn's title in each hymnal side by side. For master-index rows, `hymn_titles` / `hymn_titles_by_hymnal` likewise keep every known title (the row's own HYMN TITLE and Alternative English Titles, KELG German, WH, ELHB, TLHy, TLH, LW, ELH, LSB, TL, LPC, ELGBS's German by LPC number, WS'69 and WS'00) rather than a single one. The `hymn_catalog` view puts it all on one row: every hymnal's number, the German (KELG or ELGBS) title, the English title in the LCMS index and its alternative titles, WH, ELHB, TLHy, TLH, LW, ELH, LSB, TL, LPC, ELGBS, WS'69 and WS'00, and the original title and language and other English title, with nulls where a title isn't known.

**ELHB Hymn Index.xlsx** has all the ELHB hymns by number and name (as they appear in ELHB). Checked against the ELHB itself (a [1927 printing](https://archive.org/details/evangelicalluthe0000vari_b7v6), where its OCR text allows: about 500 of the 594), it swaps two pairs of numbers, 230/286 and 431/461; `build_db.py` corrects them (`ELHB_INDEX_FIXES`).

**LCMS-Worship-Lutheran-Hymn-Index.xlsx** has every hymn in TLH, LW, LBW, LSB, CW'93, CW'21, CWS, HS'98, ELH, and WOV and their corresponding number in each hymnal, now also every KELG, ELHB, TLHy, TL and LPC hymn (the TL# and LPC# columns, after ELHB#; a TL number can carry a letter, e.g. `157a`, or be two numbers, `202, 203`) and every hymn of TLH's two supplements, WS'69 and WS'00 (the WS'69# and WS'00# columns, after TLH#; a WS'69 number can carry Hymnary's letter for a part of a hymn, e.g. `718a`). It has one row per hymn: the LCMS Commission on Worship gave each alternate title of a hymn a row of its own, with the same hymnal numbers (*Drawn to the Cross, which Thou hast Blest* and *Drawn to the Cross which You have Blest*), and those rows are now merged (same_hymn_decisions.csv), 2547 rows into 2147. A merged row keeps the title LSB gives the hymn (else TLH, else ELH; see the decisions), and its other titles are in the **Alternative English Title 1, 2, ...** columns at the end of the sheet, as many as a row needs (five at present). A hymn's settings are one row too: *A Mighty Fortress* has LW `297, 298`. There is no ELGBS# column: ELGBS numbers its house hymnal as LPC does, and its German first lines are in elgbs_lpc_hymns.csv and the KELG/ELGBS German Title column. After the hymnal numbers come five text columns: **KELG/ELGBS German Title**, the hymn's German title in KELG or, for a hymn of ELGBS's house hymnal that KELG lacks, ELGBS's German first line (the house hymnal was a supplement to KELG in all but name; where KELG has the hymn too, its title stands, with ELGBS's beside it where ELGBS words the first line differently: KELG 330, which ELGBS opens *In Gottes Namen fahren wir*, and KELG 444, *Gott Lob, der Sonntag ist herbei*); **Original Title** and **Original Language**, the original (Norwegian, Danish, Latin, Greek, German not in KELG or ELGBS, ...) of a TLHy or TL hymn that isn't a KELG or ELGBS hymn, or just its language when only that is known; and **Other English Title** and **Other English Source**, an English translation of a KELG 1917-supplement hymn found outside these hymnals (Matthew Carver's Hymnoglypt, Hymnary, CCEL), which is also the row's HYMN TITLE (the German stays in KELG/ELGBS German Title). A TLHy row with none of these is an English original, apart from 7 translations whose original hasn't been traced yet (hymnal.db's `tlhy_sorting` lists them). Then come the Alternative English Titles: the titles of the rows merged into this one, then the English titles the hymnals give the hymn (WH, TLHy, ELHB, TL, LPC, and TLH, LW, ELH and LSB through tlh_1941.csv and the KELG index) where they differ in wording: *Thee* for *You* (*Come unto me, you weary* beside *Come unto Me, Ye Weary*), or other words altogether (ELHB's *Yes, there remaineth yet a rest* for *A Rest Remaineth for the Weary*), but not a mere difference in spelling or punctuation or a scanning error in an index. Titles typed in by hand stay (master_corrections.csv can list more); `write_back.py` only ever adds to them, except to take out one that repeats the row's title (as after a retitle).

**WH.pdf** is a PDF of Walther's Hymnal (WH), the English translation of KELG. WH numbers are KELG numbers (1–443; the 1917 supplement, KELG 444–485, isn't in it).

**TLHy.pdf** is a scan of The Lutheran Hymnary (1913) from the Internet Archive. Its hymns are numbered 1–618; the index of first lines is on PDF pp. 772–781 (printed pp. 670–679).

**The Lutheran Hymnal (1941).pdf** (page images only) and **Lutheran Service Book (2006).pdf** (with an OCR text layer) are scans for checking hymn numbers and titles. LSB's hymns end at 966; the master index's LSB 967–986 (20 rows) are hymns in LSB's accompaniment edition that no pew edition prints, an expansion of the standard LSB, so there is no index to check them against.

**Te Laudamus/** holds photos of Te Laudamus's index of hymn titles (Hymns1–3.png, printed pp. 533–535). Te Laudamus (TL) has all 150 Psalms and numbers its hymns 151–322; letters distinguish items that share a number (157a/157b, 312a/b).

**te_laudamus_index.csv** is that index: hymn number, title, `alternate` (1 for a cross-reference entry: *Nicene Creed*, 236) and the index page, one row per number, so an entry printed with two numbers ('202, 203', '312a/b') has two rows, each noting how it was printed. It was transcribed from the photos and checked against two tesseract OCR passes (the eight numbers OCR could not read were checked on enlarged crops). Its 175 entries cover every hymn from 151 to 322: 178 numbers, counting 157a/b, 213a/b, 225a/b, 312a/b, 319a/b and 322a/b separately. The index published on [telaudamus.org](https://www.telaudamus.org/contents) ([Index PDF](https://www.telaudamus.org/s/Index-pdf.pdf), printed pp. 526–533, so another printing) differs in places: it has *When My Last Hour Is Close at Hand* at 264 and *Day of Wrath* at 265, and words some titles differently (291 *In Thee, Lord, have I put My Trust*, 219 *The Day Hath Dawned—the Day of Days*); te_laudamus_index.csv follows the photos.

**tl_originals.csv** records, like tlhy_originals.csv, the originals of the TL hymns whose titles matched nothing (and of a few placed by decision), mostly from TL's own indexes of authors and translators and of hymn tunes in that Index PDF, with Bacon's edition of Luther's hymns ([Dr. Martin Luther's Deutsche Geistliche Lieder](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/417), 1883) for Massie's translations and Julian for the Latin. TL draws on Walther's Hymnal, *A New Song* (1975), TLH, ELH, LW and *Liber Hymnorum*, and adds hymns by Martin Franzmann and translations by Matthew Carver.

**Evangelisch_Lutherischer_Gebets_Schatz.pdf** is the Evangelisch-Lutherischer Gebets-Schatz (ELGBS), the Missouri Synod's prayer book (St. Louis, 1864; a Google Books scan of the 15th edition, 1881). After its numbered prayers comes a *Hausgesangbüchlein* of 106 hymns for the home (scan pp. 482–549), which its preface says holds only hymns not already in KELG; a few later went into KELG's 1917 supplement. **LPC_Int_Aug12018.pdf** is the Lutheran Prayer Companion (LPC, 2018), its English translation, mostly by Matthew Carver: its hymn section translates the house hymnal under the same numbers, 1–106. Both books also quote hymn stanzas within their prayers; those aren't indexed.

**elgbs_lpc_hymns.csv** lists that house hymnal: number, German first line (ELGBS, as LPC's index of German first lines spells it, completed where it shortens one), LPC's English first line, author and translator (LPC's credit lines), tune, page in each book, a `row_title` where the first line alone would mislead (35 opens like Herman's *Lobt Gott, ihr Christen allzugleich* but is a different hymn), and notes. It was taken from LPC's text layer and checked against the ELGBS scan's OCR, where every number turns up with its first line.

**WS69.html** and **WS2000.html** are Hymnary.org's tables of the hymns in TLH's two supplements: the Worship Supplement of the LCMS Commission on Worship (1969, WS'69) and Worship Supplement 2000 (WS'00), compiled and edited by John C. Reim of Immanuel Lutheran College, Eau Claire, Wisconsin (Church of the Lutheran Confession). Both number their hymns from 701, after TLH's: WS'69 701–793, where Hymnary letters the parts of the Quempas Celebration (718a–c) and of the Victimae Paschali Celebration (741a–b), one per tune, and WS'00 701–800. **WS69 hymn index.pdf** is a scan of WS'69's own Index of First Lines and Titles (printed pp. 251–253). It agrees with Hymnary's table on every number; at 747 it prints *Thy strong arm did cleave the darkness* for Franzmann's *Thy strong word*.

**ws69_index.csv** and **ws2000_index.csv** are made from those tables by `extract_ws.py`: number, first line, tune, `alternate` (1 for another first line WS'69's index gives a part of a hymn: *Christ is arisen* for 741b, *The glorious angels came today* and *God's own Son is born a child* for the Quempas parts, *Now the silence* for 770) and a note. In hymnal.db they are the `ws_index` table, and the `ws_status` view shows each hymn's rows.

**tlhy_index.csv** is that index of first lines: hymn number, first line and translator, one row per index entry (619 entries; hymn 452 is indexed twice, the second entry marked `alternate`). It was transcribed from the page images and checked against two independent OCR passes; the numbers cover 1–618 exactly once each. In hymnal.db it is the `tlhy_index` table; the `tlhy_status` view shows each TLHy hymn as matched or needing review, and the `tlhy_sorting` table sorts each one as a KELG hymn, a translation from another language (with its original), an English original, or a translation whose original wasn't traced.

**tlhy_originals.csv** records what TLHy's translated hymns were translated from: language, original first line, author, a confidence (`confirmed` when a source ties this English text to the original, `probable` on strong indirect evidence, `partial` when only the language and author are known), the source, and a note. Sources are Julian's *Dictionary of Hymnology* (1907; [vol. 1](https://archive.org/details/dictionaryofhymn01lond), [vol. 2](https://archive.org/details/dictionaryofhymn02lond)), the author lines printed under each hymn in TLHy.pdf, the TLH 1941 notes (tlh_1941.csv), and pages found through Hymnary, CCEL, Christ My Song and others. Where the research found the very same translation under another hymnal's number, `same_as` says so (e.g. `ELHB 456`). Hymnary.org itself blocks automated access, so its data was used only as it appears in web search results.

**tlh_1941.csv** is made by `extract_tlh.py` from Project Wittenberg's public-domain text edition of The Lutheran Hymnal (1941): each hymn's title, first line, author, translator, tune, and the original first line its notes give. It is plain ASCII, so umlauts appear as ae/oe/ue. About 40 hymns still under copyright in 1941 are missing, and tlh251.txt repeats hymn 252, so 251 is left out. In hymnal.db it is `tlh_hymns`; its titles are among each row's TLH titles.

**kelg_english.csv** lists English translations of KELG 1917-supplement hymns (which WH lacks), with translator, source and link: mostly Matthew Carver's own translations on his blog Hymnoglypt (matthaeusglyptes.blogspot.com), plus a few older ones from Hymnary and CCEL. Seven of the 23 have none anywhere yet: KELG 452, 456, 457, 466, 472, 474 and 477; LPC (Carver) translates 444 and 468. The 16 with a translation are titled with it in the master index; the seven stay titled in German until one is added here.

**wh_index.csv** is an index of WH.pdf made by `extract_wh.py`: for each hymn its English and German first lines (from the book's first-line indexes), tune, and the tune settings it points to (e.g. `TLH 237; LW 215; LSB 947`, `*` = substitute tune). It holds index data only, not hymn texts, and can be hand-corrected; `build_db.py` loads it.

**hymnal.db** is a SQLite database generated by `build_db.py` from all three spreadsheets. It holds the master index, the KELG and ELHB indexes, and the links between them (table `links`, view `proposed_index`). The spreadsheets are the source of truth; run `python3 build_db.py` to regenerate the database after editing any of them.

**link_review.csv** (also generated by `build_db.py`) lists KELG/ELHB/TLHy/TL/LPC/WS'69/WS'00 links that need a human look: number disagreements between the indexes, near-miss title matches, and numbers in the master index that the ELHB index titles differently. Hymns that match nothing are listed there as `unmatched` until `write_back.py` gives them rows of their own. It also lists, as `same hymn?`, two rows that share a hymnal number (page numbers aside) that same_hymn_decisions.csv neither merges nor keeps apart. It is empty at present.

**link_decisions.csv** settles such links: hymnal, number, the master-index row's title, `match` or `no match`, and the reason with its evidence. `build_db.py` applies it to every row with that title (as its HYMN TITLE, else as an Alternative English Title; a title merged into a row, or corrected in master_corrections.csv, still finds it): a `match` makes the link clean (adding it if no route found it) and a `no match` sets it to `rejected`; `write_back.py` then adds the number to the row's cell or takes it out. A decision naming a row that doesn't exist yet (one `write_back.py` is about to add) is skipped with a warning.

**master_corrections.csv** lists hymnal numbers and titles the master index has wrong, with the evidence (the hymnal itself or its index; e.g. LSB 878 is *Abide with Me*, so *Stay with Us* is LSB 879). `write_back.py` takes the old numbers out of the cell and puts the new ones in (a correction with no new number just takes the old one out); a cell that no longer holds the old numbers is left alone. A correction whose hymnal is `title` fixes the HYMN TITLE itself (*Tender Shepherd Thou has Stilled* → *hast*), and one whose hymnal is `alternate` gives the row an Alternative English Title, `new` (LW's three seasonal first lines of *Hail Thee, Festival Day*); one whose hymnal is `german` sets the KELG/ELGBS German Title of the row still holding `old` (KELG 330 with ELGBS's *In Gottes Namen fahren wir* beside KELG's *Im namen Gottes reisen wir*, and KELG 444 with *Gott Lob, der Sonntag ist herbei* beside *Gottlob, der Sonntag kommt herbei*).

**same_hymn_decisions.csv** says which master-index rows are one hymn: title, master_title, decision, reason, and `identified_by`. `same hymn` merges the row titled `title` into the row titled `master_title`, which keeps its title (`write_back.py`); `different hymns` keeps two rows that share a hymnal number or a title apart (the centos of Bernard's *De contemptu mundi*, which ELH 534 prints as one hymn; Albert's *God, Who Madest Earth and Heaven* and Heber's evening hymn; Townend's *The Lord's My Shepherd*, CW'21 554, and the Scottish Psalter's). `identified_by`, a hymnal number such as `LSB 953`, says which row is meant when several bear the title (Luther's and Clausnitzer's creed hymns both open *We All Believe in One True God*). A line with the same title twice merges the rows so titled (two settings). The merges were decided from the hymnal numbers the rows share, checked against LSB's, TLH's and ELH's indexes where the numbers disagreed; the row keeps the title LSB gives the hymn (its index of first lines), else TLH's (tlh_1941.csv), else ELH's. Making the rows one hymn turned up master-index numbers on the wrong hymn, which master_corrections.csv and link_decisions.csv now correct (e.g. LBW 143 is *Now All the Vault of Heaven Resounds*, so *Not Alone for Mighty Empire* is LBW 437; CW'93 252 is the Psalm 100 hymn, not *O Sing to the Lord*).

Titles count as the same when they differ only in capitalization, punctuation, elisions (*ev'ry*/*every*, *heav'n*/*heaven*), *O*/*Oh*, archaic forms (*Thee*, *Thou*, *Thy*, *Ye*, *hast*, *doth* → *You*, *Your*, *have*, *does*) or word breaks (*Christmas tide*/*Christmastide*). Translations of one original are one hymn and share its rows, even a very loose one (*Once in the Blest Baptismal Waters*, *Who Knows When Death May Overtake Me* and TLHy's *Who knows how near my life's expended* all come from *Wer weiß, wie nahe mir mein Ende*, KELG 429).

TLHy and TL hymns are matched first by title: a TLHy first line or TL index title (read in order, *Great "O" Antiphons, The* as *The Great "O" Antiphons*, and also without a parenthesis) equal to one of a row's known titles is a clean match, and the number then also goes on the other rows of the same hymn, i.e. rows sharing a KELG or TLH number, or an LW/LSB/ELH number and a similar title. A near-match is confirmed when the row's TLH hymn has the same translator. Then by original: a translation whose original (tlhy_originals.csv, tl_originals.csv) is a KELG hymn goes on that hymn's rows, else one whose original matches a TLH hymn's original goes on that hymn's rows, else the rows whose Original Title it is; a near-match on the same row is confirmed. Different hymns can share a first line (Rodigast's and Schmolck's *Was Gott tut, das ist wohlgetan*), so such a match stays for review.

LPC's hymns are matched the same way, by LPC's English first line and then by their German original from ELGBS. Because ELGBS meant its house hymnal to hold hymns not in KELG, every match through KELG was checked against the hymn itself (link_decisions.csv): some open with the same words as a KELG hymn and are different hymns (22, 35, 45, 66).

WS'69's and WS'00's hymns are matched the same way, by their first lines. Where a first line only begins the row's title, or words it differently, link_decisions.csv settles it (*Greet, man, the swiftly changing year* is LW's *Now Greet the Swiftly Changing Year*; *O Lord my God, when I in awesome wonder* is *How Great Thou Art*, by LSB's index of first lines).

**write_back.py** adds the TL#, LPC#, WS'69# and WS'00# columns when they are missing, takes out ELGBS# and renames KELG German Title to KELG/ELGBS German Title (columns are found by their headers, so the cells, widths, sort range and so on to their right move over), fills the master index's KELG#, TLHy#, ELHB#, TL#, LPC#, WS'69#, WS'00# and KELG/ELGBS German Title columns from the clean links, the Original Title/Language columns from tlhy_originals.csv and tl_originals.csv and the Other English columns from kelg_english.csv, adds a row for every KELG, ELHB, TLHy, TL, LPC, WS'69 or WS'00 hymn that has none yet (a KELG hymn with no other English counterpart is titled with its WH first line, a TLHy hymn with its TLHy first line, an ELHB hymn with its ELHB first line, a TL hymn with its index title, TL numbers of one title sharing a row, an LPC hymn with LPC's first line, a WS'69 or WS'00 hymn with its first line), retitles a row still titled in German with WH's English, or for a hymn WH lacks with kelg_english.csv's, and fills the WH column of KELG-Hymn-Index.xlsx. A filled number cell changes only by link_decisions.csv or master_corrections.csv. Once those are written it merges the rows same_hymn_decisions.csv calls one hymn (their numbers together, their texts where the kept row's are empty or differ, their titles as Alternative English Titles) and then adds the hymnals' titles to the Alternative English Titles, adding columns as rows need them. `--merge-kelg N,...` merges a KELG hymn's own row (titled in German or with its English title) into the row(s) the hymn was matched to. Writing can bring more links to light (rows that come to share a number are the same hymn), so it repeats until nothing changes; it is safe to re-run, e.g. after adding to link_decisions.csv.

Numbers one apart in the same hymnal (LW 297/298, *A Mighty Fortress*) are treated as two settings of one hymn rather than a disagreement.

## Goal

The end goal of the project is to complete these columns in LCMS-Worship-Lutheran-Hymn-Index.xlsx:

1. Fill in the KELG/WH, ELHB, and TLHy columns, wiring their hymns to the existing rows (three columns). Done: all 485 KELG, 594 ELHB and 618 TLHy hymns are in, every one on clean links, and link_review.csv is empty. 441 TLHy hymns are on existing rows and 177 on new rows of their own.
2. Add the German titles from KELG where relevant (one column). Done: the KELG/ELGBS German Title column, which also has ELGBS's German first lines for the 96 hymns of its house hymnal that KELG lacks.
3. Wire in Te Laudamus (TL), the first of the new sources. Done: all 178 TL numbers are on clean links, 158 on existing rows (236 rows) and 20 on 18 new rows. Of the new rows, 8 are Martin Franzmann's English hymns, 6 translations with their original first line (Latin: *Christe, Redemptor omnium*, *Credo in unum Deum*, *Ales diei nuntius*, *Verbum supernum prodiens*; German: *Sie ist mir lieb, die werte Magd*, *O Mensch, bewein dein Sünde groß*), 2 German hymns known only by author (Benjamin Prätorius 227, Albert Knapp 262), and 2 whose original hasn't been traced (163 *In Bethlehem God's Son Is Born*, 290 *What They Would Gain Men Hope to Win*). The Great O Antiphons (157b) share the *O Come, O Come, Emmanuel* rows with 157a, as LSB prints them facing hymn 357.
4. Wire in LPC and ELGBS. Done (ELGBS now only for LPC's German originals): all 106 hymns of the house hymnal are on clean links, 17 on 21 existing rows and 89 on new rows titled with LPC's first line, each with its German first line in KELG/ELGBS German Title. The 17 include ten that KELG has (KELG 330, which ELGBS prints as *In Gottes Namen fahren wir*, and nine from its 1917 supplement: 444, 451, 458, 468, 476, 479, 481, 482, 483) and hymns in TLH or TLHy such as *Ich will dich lieben, meine Stärke* and *Liebe, die du mich zum Bilde*.

5. One row per hymn, its other English titles in Alternative English Title columns. Done: 691 rows that were the same hymn under other titles, or another setting of it, merged into 291, so 2547 rows became 2147, and 340 rows have 487 alternative titles (341 from the merged rows, 143 from the hymnals, 3 from master_corrections.csv). CW'21's *From Depths of Woe, Lord God, I Cry* (650), Luther's text versified by Michael D. Schultz, is on the row of *From Depths of Woe I Cry to Thee*. The two translations of *Veni, Creator Spiritus* (Cosin's *Come, Holy Ghost, Our Souls Inspire* and Caswall's *Come, Holy Ghost, Creator Blest*) are one row, as are LW's three seasonal printings of *Hail Thee, Festival Day* (125, 148, 159, their first lines alternative titles) and CW'21 553 and 555, the Scottish Psalter's Psalm 23 to two tunes; Townend's *The Lord's My Shepherd* (CW'21 554) and Kosche's *Lamb of God* (CW'21 403) keep rows of their own. The row *Holy, Holy, Holy* turned out to be the Sanctus (CW'21 944–945) with Heber's hymn numbers added; those went to *Holy, Holy, Holy, Lord God Almighty* and the row to *Sanctus* (LSB 961). LW 492 is Heber's *God, Who Made the Earth and Heaven*, and LW 520 prints both of Boye's hymns, *He Is Arisen! Glorious Word* and *Rejoice, Rejoice This Happy Morn*, so it stays on both rows.

6. Wire in TLH's two supplements, WS'69 and WS'00. Done: all 96 WS'69 and 100 WS'00 hymns are on clean links. 86 WS'69 numbers are on 83 existing rows and 10 on new rows; the three parts of the Quempas Celebration (718a–c) share *He Whom Shepherds Once Came Praising* (LW 54), and the Victimae Paschali Celebration's two parts go to the two hymns LSB prints apart, 741a to *Christians, to the Paschal Victim* and 741b, the Leise, to *Christ Is Arisen*. 94 WS'00 hymns are on existing rows (*Jerusalem, Jerusalem*, 794, is *Jerusalem, My Happy Home*, the F. B. P. text rendered anew) and 6 on new rows; 18 rows have numbers in both.

TLHy's 618 hymns sort as 127 KELG hymns, 290 English originals, and 194 translations from other originals: German not in KELG 83, Latin 40, Danish 40, Norwegian 16, Greek 8, Swedish 3, French 2, Italian 1, Dutch 1. For 7 more translations the original hasn't been traced. Of the 177 TLHy hymns new to the master index, 97 are English originals and 80 translations (68 with their original first line, 12 with only the language).
