# The General Prayer of the Church: Texts by Family

The general prayer (*das gemein Gebet*, the Prayer of the Church) is the intercession for all
estates and needs in the chief Sunday service of the church orders in the Sehling corpus. This
guide does two things:

- §§1–3 say what the general prayer is, where it stands in the service, and how its texts are
  related.
- §§4–21 give the texts themselves, one family at a time. For each petition the original is
  followed by the English, for the rubric, the bid and the prayer in turn. Each family can be
  read straight through, in the order in which the prayer runs.

The texts are the **representative texts** of `general_prayers.xlsx`. They come from four sheets
of the workbook:

- **Orig Prayers - Representative**;
- **Eng Prayers - Representative**;
- **Orig Bids - Representative**;
- **Eng Bids - Representative**.

The workbook sets these out with one row per category and one column per family, so that one
intention can be compared across the families. Here they are turned around: each family's column
is printed as a single prayer.

[GENERAL_PRAYERS_GUIDE.md](GENERAL_PRAYERS_GUIDE.md) describes the workbook and
`general_prayers.db`: how the texts were curated and checked against the corpus, the categories,
the critical texts and how the representative texts are made. Use it to find:

- every witness's own text, in the **Prayers** sheet;
- the variants within a family, in the **Archetypes** sheet and the critical sheets;
- the queries that compare the orders.

**Conventions**

- **Original language.** The text of Sehling's edition, with his apparatus removed. Spelling is
  as printed. "N." stands where the order leaves a name to be filled in. "etc." stands where the
  order gives only the opening of a well-known text (the Our Father, the Creed).
- **English.** A formal-equivalence translation in the idiom of the Authorized Version
  (thee/thou, -eth), keeping the word order and clause structure where English allows.
- **Rubric.** A heading or direction printed with the petition. **Bid.** Words addressed to the
  people ("Let us pray for …"). **Prayer.** Words addressed to God.
- **Witnesses.** The church orders that carry a family's text. Each is named by place and year,
  as in the workbook.

## 1. The general prayer in the Sunday service

### 1.1 What it is

Every order in this set (57 witnesses, dated 1526–1618) has one prayer in the chief service of
the Sunday in which the congregation prays for the Church and her ministers, the magistrates,
the afflicted and the other needs of Christendom. The orders call it:

- *das gemein gebet*, the common prayer. This is the usual name: Württemberg 1553 has "Ordnung
  des gemeinen gebets", Leipzig 1567 "Gemein gebet".
- *Vermanung zum gebet*, the exhortation to prayer: Hessen 1566 (Moderate Reformed), Strasbourg
  1598. Nürnberg 1545 has "Vermanung zu bitten für allerlei stende".
- *Fürbitt* or *Vorbitt*, intercession: Augsburg 1537 (Moderate Reformed; Sehling's heading),
  Öhringen 1544.
- *Exhortatio edder vormaninge*, exhortation or admonition: Braunschweig 1528, Hamburg 1529.
- *Oratio*: the Latin order of the French church at Frankfurt, 1554 (Reformed).

Hessen 1566 (Moderate Reformed) gives it as the fifth part of the service and grounds it in the
practice of the first Church:

> Von solchem gebet, so uf die auslegung der schrift und predigt gefolget hat in der ersten
> kirchen, redet sehr kurz Iustinus in der andern Apologia, da er spricht: Post exhortationes ab
> eo qui praeest factas, surgimus omnes ac comprecamur, das ist, wenn die predigt vom öbersten
> lehrer volendet, stehen wir alle uf und beten. (Sehling 8, p. 245)

> Of such prayer, which in the first Church followed upon the exposition of the Scripture and
> the sermon, Justin speaketh very briefly in his second Apology, where he saith: *After the
> exhortations made by him that presideth, we all rise and pray together*; that is, when the
> sermon of the chief teacher is ended, we all stand up and pray.

What counts as the general prayer, and what is left out (collect banks, the Litany, prayer-day
and afternoon forms), is set out in GENERAL_PRAYERS_GUIDE.md, *What counts as "the general
prayer"*.

### 1.2 Where it stands in the service

**After the sermon.** In 48 of the 57 witnesses the prayer follows the sermon, and in most of
them it is said from the pulpit. Kursachsen 1580 has it said "allezeit nach der predigt … dem
volk auf der canzel" (always after the sermon … to the people from the pulpit). In these orders
the prayer is the hinge between the service of the Word and the Supper, and it is often joined
to the items around it:

- **Before it, in many orders:** an open confession and absolution, or the publishing of banns
  and notices. Examples:
  - confession and absolution in Neckarbischofsheim 1560, Erbach 1560, Hohenlohe 1578, the
    Ysenburg orders, Braunschweig-Wolfenbüttel 1569, Oldenburg 1573 and Kurpfalz 1563
    (Reformed);
  - banns and notices in Hohenlohe 1578, Solms-Laubach 1576, Strasbourg 1598 and Frankfurt 1554
    (Reformed).
- **After it:** the Our Father, which closes almost every form. Then:
  - a psalm or hymn, such as "Erhalt uns Herr", "Verleih uns Frieden" or "Danck sagen wir alle";
  - a collect and the blessing when there is no Communion;
  - otherwise the Supper. In Lüneburg 1564 and its followers the communicants come into the
    choir and kneel.

**Elsewhere in the service.** The other nine witnesses place it as follows:

| Place | Witnesses |
|---|---|
| In the Mass, said by the priest in German under the Sanctus, before or after the Words of Institution | Brandenburg 1540, Calenberg-Göttingen 1542, Pfalz-Neuburg 1543 (family L) |
| In the Communion office, after the exhortation to the communicants, before the Words of Institution | Frankfurt 1553 and 1589 |
| After the Kyrie and Gloria, in place of the Latin collect, read from a lectern before the altar (the sermon precedes the whole service) | Schwäbisch Hall 1526 |
| In place of the Epistle in the village Mass, followed by the Creed, the Ten Commandments and the catechism (the medieval pulpit office) | Nürnberg 1545 |
| After the Gospel has been expounded from the pulpit and the catechism recited, before the Creed and Preface | Osnabrück 1543 |
| From the pulpit, when the choir has finished the first psalm or hymn | Worms 1560 |

The Brandenburg prayers keep the place of the old intercessions of the Canon. Sehling notes on
Pfalz-Neuburg 1543:

> Diese Gebete sind an die Stelle der in der römischen Messe teils vor teils nach der
> Konsekration gesprochenen Fürbittgebete gesetzt. Sie wurden sonst in evangelischen
> Kirchenordnungen ohne Ersatz gestrichen, weil sie den von der Reformation abgelehnten
> Opfercharakter der Messe zur Grundlage haben. (Sehling 13, p. 74)

> These prayers are set in the place of the intercessory prayers spoken in the Roman Mass partly
> before and partly after the consecration. Elsewhere in the evangelical church orders these
> were struck out without replacement, because they rest upon the sacrificial character of the
> Mass, which the Reformation rejected.

**Who says it.** The minister says it: the pastor or the deacon from the pulpit, or the priest
at the altar in family L. In Schwäbisch Hall 1526 it is read from a lectern before the altar, in
a cope. The people take part:

- by saying the Our Father;
- by the confession, which they say after the minister in Braunschweig ("spreket ock de bicht
  mit my") and in Ysenburg-Ronneburg 1582 ("Bekennet mit mir vor Gott ewer sunde");
- by praying "mit mund und herzen" (with mouth and heart), as the Leipzig print of 1567 directs.

### 1.3 Its forms

The witnesses use six forms. Each family keeps to one form, except for the two prescriptions of
contents: Magdeburg 1562 (in B2) and Osnabrück 1543 (in H).

| Form | How it runs | Families | Witnesses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bidding and collect | For each intention the minister bids the people ("Lasst uns bitten für …"), then says a collect | A, B1 | 10 |
| Bidding exhortation | The minister tells the people what to pray for, item by item, without addressing God, and closes with the Our Father | C, D, G, H, I, J, M | 14 |
| Continuous prayer | One prayer to God that names the intentions in turn ("Wöllest auch …") | B2, E, F, R1, R3, R4 | 25 |
| Collects | Separate collects without biddings: three from the pulpit (K), or the priest's collects under the Sanctus (L) | K, L | 4 |
| Lord's Prayer paraphrase | The intentions are arranged under the petitions of the Our Father | R2 (and the close of R3) | 2 |
| Prescription of contents | Not a text to be read, but a list in indirect speech of what the prayer must contain | Magdeburg 1562 (B2), Osnabrück 1543 (H) | 2 |

**Long and short forms.** Württemberg 1553 prints a long form (B1) and a short form (B2).
Pfalz-Zweibrücken 1557 says when each is to be used:

> Dises gebet, dieweil es zimlich lang ist, mögen die Kirchendiener etwan an den grossen Festen
> gebrauchen, als am Christag, Ostern und Pfingsten, und sonst jeder zeit im jar, wann die Kirch
> one das mit der predig nit lang auffgehalten wirt, sonst sollen sie die kürtzere form, welche
> hernach folget, gemeinigklich dem Volck fürlesen. (Sehling 18, p. 195)

> This prayer, forasmuch as it is somewhat long, the ministers of the Church may use at the
> great feasts, as on Christmas Day, Easter and Pentecost, and at any other time in the year
> when the church is not otherwise held long by the sermon; else they shall commonly read to the
> people the shorter form, which followeth hereafter.

The short form became the most copied prayer in the corpus (17 witnesses, 1553–1618).

**Alternatives and freedom.** Some orders allow the prayer to vary:

- **The Litany instead.** The Litany is the second form of the common prayer in Württemberg 1553
  and Rothenburg 1559. It is prayed on Fridays in the towns of Kursachsen 1580 and on alternate
  Sundays in Rothenberg 1618.
- **A choice of texts.** Kurpfalz 1563 (Reformed) offers a Lord's-Prayer paraphrase, "Oder also"
  (or thus), beside its Sunday prayer (R2). Augsburg 1537 (Moderate Reformed) offers two texts
  against the Turk.
- **Local adaptation.**
  - Hessen 1566 (Moderate Reformed) lets each church make the form "kürzer oder lenger" (shorter
    or longer).
  - Hanau-Lichtenberg 1573 tells the pastor to refer to the sermon and to present needs.
  - Bentheim-Tecklenburg 1588 (Reformed) lets ministers add to the prayer in time of need.
  - Many forms end with a rubric that lets the sick and other particular needs be named (the
    category *Special intercessions*).

### 1.4 The sequence of intentions

**Lutheran.** The usual Lutheran series goes back to Brenz 1526 and Württemberg 1553:

exhortation > Church > magistrates > afflicted > peace > enemies > women with child > fruits of
the earth > Our Father.

**Reformed.** The Heidelberg prayer of 1563 begins with confession and thanksgiving, prays for
the fruit of the Word, and has a petition for all men (1 Tim 2:1):

confession > thanksgiving > Word > magistrates > fruits of the earth > all men > persecuted >
afflicted > Our Father.

**What all share.** Civil authority is the one intention found in every witness.
GENERAL_PRAYERS_GUIDE.md, *What the comparison shows*, sets out the other patterns.

**Traditions.**

The traditions follow the inventory in [`CHURCH_ORDERS_GUIDE.md`](CHURCH_ORDERS_GUIDE.md), §3.
Every order of another tradition than the Lutheran is marked where it is cited, by its tradition
in brackets after it, as "Kurpfalz 1563 (Reformed)".
- Eleven families are Lutheran: A to E and H to L.
- F (Augsburg 1537) is Moderate Reformed: Upper German and Bucerian.
- G (Hessen 1566) is Moderate Reformed: the church of Philip of Hesse, between Wittenberg and
  the Upper Germans, which bore Bucer's stamp.
- M (Norden 1528) is an early East Frisian evangelical order, which Sehling prints among the
  Reformed orders of East Frisia; it is marked Reformed here.
- R1–R4 are Reformed. Schaumburg 1614, a Lutheran order, takes over the Heidelberg prayer of R1.
- No Interim order is among the witnesses.

## 2. How the texts are given here

**Representative texts.** A family's representative text is one clean text for each category,
made from all the family's witnesses by majority:

- a petition, bid or prayer is kept if at least half the witnesses to that category have it;
- word by word, the reading of the majority is chosen;
- where no reading has half the witnesses, the earliest witness's reading stands.

Every word comes from a witness, but in a family with several witnesses the result need not
match any one witness exactly. Where a family has a single witness, its text is that witness's
text. The rules are given in full in GENERAL_PRAYERS_GUIDE.md, *Representative texts*.

**Order.** The workbook lists the categories in a standard order. Here each family's petitions
follow the order in which its prayer actually runs:

- the order of the family's earliest witness;
- with petitions that only later witnesses have placed where those witnesses have them.

Where a family has more than one petition in a category (two petitions for the magistrates in
Lüneburg 1564, or the confession and the absolution), the workbook cell holds them both,
separated by a blank line. Here each is given in its own place.

**Exact reproduction.** The petitions of each family, joined category by category, reproduce the
four workbook sheets exactly. This was checked for all 2,016 cells (28 categories × 18 families
× 4 sheets), with no differences. The workbook's "Bids" sheets put the rubric on the first line
of the bid; here the rubric and the bid are given separately.

**Each petition** is numbered within its family (A.1, A.2, …) and headed with its category. For
families with more than one witness, a line in italics names the witnesses that have the
petition. It also notes where a rubric, bid or prayer is in only some of them. Then come, in
turn:

- the rubric, original then English;
- the bid, original then English;
- the prayer, original then English.

Each part is given only where the petition has it.

**Not taken in.** Each family section lists the witness petitions that are not in the
representative text, because another reading of the same category has the majority. Those texts
are in the **Prayers** sheet and in the critical sheets.

## 4. Family A: Brenz bidding prayer

Deacon or pastor bids the people for one intention ("Lasst uns bitten für …"), then says a
collect for it; the series runs Church, magistrates, peace, afflicted, all needs, and closes
with the Lord's Prayer. Frankfurt adds collects from Brandenburg-Nürnberg 1533 and Veit
Dietrich.

- **Earliest witness (archetype):** Schwäbisch Hall 1526 (Brenz).
- **Tradition:** Lutheran.
- **Language:** German.
- **Form:** bidding + collect.
- **Place in the service (Schwäbisch Hall 1526):** After the Kyrie and Gloria, in place of the
  Latin Oremus/collect; read from a lectern before the altar in a cope. The sermon precedes the
  whole service.

| Year | Witness | Territory | Sehling | Form |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1526 | Schwäbisch Hall, Frühmessordnung (Johannes Brenz) | Schwäbisch Hall (Imperial city) | Sehling 17, I/1, pp. 37–40 | bidding + collect |
| 1543 | Schwäbisch Hall, Kirchenordnung 1543 | Schwäbisch Hall (Imperial city) | Sehling 17, I/10, pp. 142–145 | bidding + collect |
| 1553 | Frankfurt am Main, Agende 1553 („Einfeltige form, das Nachtmal des Herren zuhalten“) | Frankfurt am Main (Imperial city) | Sehling 9, VI/Frankfurt Nr. 10, pp. 524–526 | bidding + collect |
| 1560 | Neckarbischofsheim, Kirchenordnung (Herren von Helmstatt) | Lordship of Neckarbischofsheim (Kraichgau) | Sehling 16, V, pp. 670–672 | bidding + collect |
| 1589 | Frankfurt am Main, Agende 1589 | Frankfurt am Main (Imperial city) | Sehling 9, VI/Frankfurt Nr. 13, pp. 552–553 | bidding + collect |

The representative text follows Brenz's Schwäbisch Hall form of 1526, which the later witnesses
copy with small changes. Where they part from it, as in the preface and the close, its reading
stands. Following the family's own majority, each bid ends "Bittend also:" and the collects end
bare (see GENERAL_PRAYERS_GUIDE.md, *Representative texts*, rule 4).

A.9 to A.13 (the Our Father, Creed and Decalogue printed as part of the prayer, and the closing
rubric) stand in Schwäbisch Hall 1526 (A.9 also in 1543). A.14 is Frankfurt 1589's own closing
prayer for the communicants and A.15 Neckarbischofsheim 1560's closing word of assurance. They
come at the end, after the Our Father, as in those orders.

**Not in the representative text** (a minority reading of its category; see the Prayers sheet or
the critical sheets): Frankfurt 1553 no. 1 (Exhortation / preface); Frankfurt 1553 no. 7 (Lord's
Prayer); Neckarbischofsheim 1560 no. 1 (Exhortation / preface); Neckarbischofsheim 1560 no. 9
(Lord's Prayer); Frankfurt 1589 no. 1 (Exhortation / preface); Frankfurt 1589 no. 7 (Lord's
Prayer).

**Sequence:** A.1 Exhortation / preface > A.2 The Church > A.3 Civil authority > A.4 The
afflicted > A.5 Peace > A.6 Enemies > A.7 Women with child > A.8 Fruits of the earth > A.9
Lord's Prayer > A.10 Lord's Prayer > A.11 Creed > A.12 Ten Commandments > A.13 Special
intercessions > A.14 Communicants > A.15 Blessing.

### A.1 Exhortation / preface

*Witnesses:* Schwäbisch Hall 1526 and Schwäbisch Hall 1543.

**Rubric (German).** Vorrede

**Rubric (English).** Preface

**Bid (German).** Auff das all glaubig zubeten gereitzt wurden, so hat unser Herr Cristus dem
gebet ein ernstlich versicherung angehengkt, sprechendt: Bitten, so werden ir gewert, suchendt,
so werden ir finden, klopffet an, so wirt euch auffgethon.

**Bid (English).** To the end that all the faithful might be stirred up to pray, our Lord Christ
hath annexed unto prayer an earnest assurance, saying: Ask, and ye shall be granted; seek, and
ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you.

### A.2 The Church

*Witnesses:* Schwäbisch Hall 1526, Schwäbisch Hall 1543, Frankfurt 1553, Neckarbischofsheim 1560
and Frankfurt 1589.

**Bid (German).** Hieruff lassent uns fleyssig bitten fur die gemein cristenlich kirch, fur der
kirchen diener, das sie unser Herr Got wolle beschutzen wider all anleuff und versuchung des
boßen feinds, darmit sie auff dem starcken felsen, unserm Herrn Cristo, bestendig pleyben.
Bittend also:

**Bid (English).** Hereupon let us diligently pray for the common Christian Church, and for the
ministers of the Church, that the Lord our God would defend them against all the assaults and
temptation of the wicked enemy, that they may abide steadfast upon the strong rock, our Lord
Christ. Pray ye thus:

**Prayer (German).** Almechtiger, ewiger Got, der du hast allen volckern dein gnad durch Jesum
Cristum und sein Evangelion geoffenbaret, erhalt, Herr, das volck deiner barmhertzikait, das
dein kirch sampt iren dienern, inn der gantzen welt zerstrewt, dir mit rechtem glauben diene und
in bekantnus deins namens bestande.

**Prayer (English).** Almighty, everlasting God, who hast revealed thy grace unto all peoples
through Jesus Christ and his Gospel: preserve, O Lord, the people of thy mercy, that thy Church
together with her ministers, scattered abroad in the whole world, may serve thee with right
faith and continue in the confession of thy name.

### A.3 Civil authority

*Witnesses:* Schwäbisch Hall 1526, Schwäbisch Hall 1543, Frankfurt 1553, Neckarbischofsheim 1560
and Frankfurt 1589.

**Bid (German).** Lassendt uns auch bitten auß gebot und bevelh unsers Herrn Jesu Cristi und
seiner Apposteln fur unsern allergnedigsten Herrn, den kaiser, fur alle konig und amptlewt des
weltlichen Regiments, sonderlich fur ein erbare Oberkait diser stat, auff das wir ein geruwigs
und stils leben in aller gotselikait furen mogen. Bittend also:

**Bid (English).** Let us also pray, by the commandment and bidding of our Lord Jesus Christ and
of his apostles, for our most gracious Lord the Emperor, for all kings and officers of the
temporal government, and especially for an honourable magistracy of this city, that we may lead

## 5. Family B1: Württemberg long form

Brenz's bidding-plus-collect scheme in its ducal form: a preface on prayer (Mt 18:19, 1 Tim 2),
then biddings each followed by a collect; used chiefly on high feasts. Spread through
Pfalz-Neuburg 1554 to the Palatinate, Zweibrücken, Veldenz and Leiningen.

- **Earliest witness (archetype):** Württemberg 1553, long form.
- **Tradition:** Lutheran.
- **Language:** German.
- **Form:** preface, then bidding + collect for each intention.
- **Place in the service (Württemberg 1553):** On Sundays and feast days immediately after the
  sermon ("auff die Sontag unnd andere Feyertag gleich nach der predig"); the Litany is the second
  form of the common prayer.

1556's lead-in to the open confession and absolution, which that order places here. B1.3 and
B1.4 are Pfalz-Veldenz 1574's own confession and absolution.

### B1.1 Exhortation / preface

*Witnesses:* Württemberg 1553, Kurpfalz 1556, Pfalz-Zweibrücken 1557, Leiningen-Westerburg 1566
and Pfalz-Veldenz 1574.

**Rubric (German).** Die vorrede des gmeinen Gebets.

**Rubric (English).** The preface of the common prayer.

**Bid (German).** Nach dem wir beieinander in gottes namen versamlet seind und uns bevolhen ist,
das wir Gott in aller unser nott sollen anruffen, auch für meniglich unser fürbitt thun, zu
welchem wir die zusagung unsers lieben Herrn Christi haben: Wa zwen under euch eins werden auff
erden, warumb es ist, das sie bitten wöllen, das soll inen von meinem vatter im himmel
widerfaren, und: Bittet, so würdt euch gegeben, suchet, so werdet ir finden, klopfft an, so
würdt euch auffgethon.

**Bid (English).** Forasmuch as we are gathered together in God’s name, and it is commanded us
that we should call upon God in all our need and also make our intercession for every man,
whereunto we have the promise of our dear Lord Christ: Where two of you shall agree on earth as
touching any thing that they shall ask, it shall be done for them of my Father which is in
heaven; and: Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be
opened unto you.

### B1.2 Confession of sins

*Witnesses:* Kurpfalz 1556.

**Bid (German).** Hierauf, dieweil wir für dem thron göttlicher majestat mit tröstlichem
gewissen und sicherem glauben in unserm gebet nicht kommen mögen noch dörfen, wir seyen dann
zuvor mit Gott versönet und von ime mit genaden aufgenommen, wir kummen aber durch keinen andern
weg zur versönung mit Gott, dann so wir unsere sünde erkhennen und glauben, das uns die sünde
von wegen des eingebornen sons Gottes, unsers lieben herren Jesu Christi, vergeben und
nachgelassen sein, so lasst uns anfencklich unsere sünde vor Gott bekhennen und hernach die
absolution von den sünden empfahen, sprechend also: Ich armer sünder etc. ut supra.

**Bid (English).** Hereupon, forasmuch as we neither can nor may come before the throne of the
divine majesty in our prayer with a comforted conscience and sure faith, except we be first
reconciled with God and received of him into grace; and we come by no other way unto
reconciliation with God, but that we acknowledge our sins and believe that our sins are forgiven
and remitted us for the sake of the only-begotten Son of God, our dear Lord Jesus Christ: let us
therefore first confess our sins before God and thereafter receive the absolution from our sins,
saying thus: I, poor sinner, etc., as above.

## 6. Family B2: Württemberg short form

The same intentions compressed into a single continuous prayer ("Allmächtiger, ewiger Gott,
himmlischer Vater …") after a brief exhortation; the ordinary Sunday form. The most widely
copied general prayer in the corpus, reaching Saxony (1580), Magdeburg, Strasbourg, Sayn, Nassau
and the Ysenburg counties.

- **Earliest witness (archetype):** Württemberg 1553, short form.
- **Tradition:** Lutheran.
- **Language:** German.
- **Form:** exhortation + one continuous prayer.
- **Place in the service (Württemberg 1553):** After the sermon, when time does not allow the
  long form to be read.

## 7. Family C: Bidding exhortation (Strasbourg type)

A bidding exhortation in the Strasbourg manner: the minister tells the people what to pray for,
item by item, without addressing God, and ends with the Our Father.

- **Earliest witness (archetype):** Hanau-Lichtenberg 1573.
- **Tradition:** Lutheran.
- **Language:** German.
- **Form:** bidding exhortation + Our Father.
- **Place in the service (Hanau-Lichtenberg 1573):** After the sermon; then a psalm is sung by
  school and congregation, followed by a collect and the blessing. The pastor is to refer to the
  sermon just preached and to present needs (war, plague, dearth) in the common prayer.

| Year | Witness | Territory | Sehling | Form |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1573 | Hanau-Lichtenberg, Kirchenordnung (Graf Philipp IV.) | County of Hanau-Lichtenberg (Alsace) | Sehling 20/2, Hanau-Lichtenberg Nr. 6, pp. 52–53 | bidding exhortation + Lord’s Prayer |

**Sequence:** C.1 Exhortation / preface > C.2 The Word & its fruit > C.3 Civil authority > C.4
All estates / households > C.5 Fruits of the earth > C.6 The afflicted > C.7 Special
intercessions > C.8 Lord's Prayer.

### C.1 Exhortation / preface

**Rubric (German).** Nach der Predig geschehe das gemein Gebet auff folgende weiß:

**Rubric (English).** After the sermon let the common prayer be made on this wise:

**Bid (German).** Lieben freund, Dieweil wir beyeinander versamlet sein und Gottes wort gehöret
haben, so wöllen wir alle not der gantzen Christenheit unserm Herrgott fürtragen, ihne bitten,
er wöll uns gnedig und barmhertzig sein, alles geben, was uns nutz und not ist, zur leibes
notturfft und zur seelen seligkeit:

**Bid (English).** Dear friends, forasmuch as we are gathered together and have heard God’s
word, we will lay all the need of the whole of Christendom before our Lord God, and pray him
that he would be gracious and merciful unto us, and give all that is profitable and needful for
us, for the necessity of the body and for the salvation of the soul:

### C.2 The Word & its fruit

**Bid (German).** Am ersten so bittet für das Geistlich, das unser lieber Herrgott uns sein
heiligs wort wölle lassen predigen und gnad verleihen, das wir solches gerne hören, mit dem
hertzen glauben und unser leben darnach richten, auch alle die jenigen, so von solchs Worts
wegen in jamer, elend und widerwertigkeit stecken, trösten, stercken und auß allem jamer und
elend erretten. Er wölle auch alle, so noch in finsternuß, irrthumb und abgötterey stecken,
gnediglich durch sein Wort und Heiligen Geist erleuchten und zu erkantnuß der warheit bringen,
auff daß sie mit uns und wir mit ihnen mögen selig werden.

**Bid (English).** First, pray ye for the spiritual estate: that our dear Lord God would let his
holy word be preached unto us, and vouchsafe grace that we may gladly hear the same, believe it
with the heart, and order our life thereafter; and that he would comfort, strengthen, and
deliver out of all misery and woe all those that for the sake of this Word are in misery, woe
and adversity. That he would also graciously enlighten by his Word and Holy Spirit all that are
yet in darkness, error and idolatry, and bring them to the knowledge of the truth, that they
with us and we with them may be saved.

### C.3 Civil authority

**Bid (German).** Zum andern bittet für das Weltlich Regiment: Erstlich für das Haupt, die
Römische Kayserliche Majestat, unsern aller gnedigsten Herren, Darnach für die Glieder, alß
Churfürsten, Fürsten und Herren der gantzen Christenheit, In sonderheit aber für unsere gnedige
Herren, Frawe und Frewlein von Hanaw, für irer Gnaden Räthe, Amptleut, Diener und die gantze
Herrschafft, für ein ersam Gericht und Gemeine alhie, auch die umbligende Nachpaurschafft. Der
Allmechtige Gott verleihe der Oberkeit seinen Heiligen Geist und weißheit, das sie Christlich
und wol regieren, die Unterthanen in gemeinem friden erhalten und bewaren.

**Bid (English).** Secondly, pray ye for the temporal government: first for the head, his Roman
Imperial Majesty, our most gracious lord; then for the members, as the Electors, princes and
lords of all Christendom; but especially for our gracious lords, lady and damsels of Hanau, for
their Graces’ counsellors, officers, servants and the whole lordship, for a worshipful court and
commune here, and also the neighbourhood round about. Almighty God vouchsafe unto the
magistrates his Holy Spirit and wisdom, that they may rule in Christian wise and well, and keep
and preserve the subjects in common peace.

### C.4 All estates / households

**Bid (German).** Letzlich bittet auch für den Ehelichen stand, der Allmechtige Gott verleihe
allen Eheleuten seinen Heiligen Geist, das sie Christlich, fridlich und eintrechtiglich
beieinander leben, ihre Kinder und Gesind zu Gottes forcht, auch ehr und tugent mögen
aufferziehen.

**Bid (English).** Lastly, pray ye also for the estate of matrimony: Almighty God vouchsafe unto
all married folk his Holy Spirit, that they may live together in Christian wise, peaceably and
in concord, and may bring up their children and household in the fear of God, and also in honour
and virtue.

### C.5 Fruits of the earth

**Bid (German).** Bittet für die frucht des Feldes, umb ein seliges Wetter und umb alles, so uns
von nöten ist.

**Bid (English).** Pray ye for the fruit of the field, for seasonable weather, and for all that

### C.6 The afflicted

**Bid (German).** Kinder und Krancken wölle unser Herrgott pflegen und warten, Schwangern und

**Bid (English).** May our Lord God tend and keep children and the sick, give unto women with

**Bid (English).** And let every man consider his own need and come with it before God, the

whole heart thus:

### C.8 Lord's Prayer

**Prayer (German).** Unser Vater, der du bist im Himel, Geheiliget werde dein Nam. Zukom dein
Reich. Dein will geschehe auff Erden wie im Himel. Unser täglich Brot gib uns heut. Und vergib

**Prayer (English).** Our Father, which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come.

For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, from everlasting to everlasting. Amen.

## 8. Family D: Nürnberg-type Vermahnung zum Gebet

"Vermahnung zum Gebet": an exhortation in the second person ("Lieben Freund, bittet …") listing
the intentions, closed by the Our Father. Copied in Waldeck, Erbach, Solms-Laubach and
Henneberg.

- **Earliest witness (archetype):** Nürnberg 1545 (Veit Dietrich).
- **Tradition:** Lutheran.
- **Language:** German.
- **Form:** bidding exhortation + Our Father.
- **Place in the service (Nürnberg 1545):** In the “Ordnung der messen auf dem land, wo nicht
  schulen sind”: after the opening German psalm the pastor reads, in place of the Epistle, this
  exhortation with the Our Father, followed by the Creed, the Ten Commandments and the catechism
  pieces on Baptism, the Keys and the Supper (the medieval pulpit office, Prone).

| 1556 | Waldeck, Kirchenordnung (Kirchen Ordnung auff den Dörffern) | County of Waldeck | Sehling 9, II/Waldeck Nr. 15 (Kirchenordnung 1556), pp. 273–274 | bidding exhortation + Lord’s Prayer |
| 1560 | Erbach, Kirchenordnung (Graf Georg) | County of Erbach | Sehling 9, IV/Erbach Nr. 6 (2. Erbacher Kirchenordnung 1560), pp. 428–429 | bidding exhortation + Lord’s Prayer |

### D.1 Exhortation / preface

*Witnesses:* Henneberg 1582.

**Rubric (German).** Nach der predigt sol der kirchendiener das volk zum gebet, fur alle stende

**Rubric (English).** After the sermon the minister shall again diligently exhort the people to

Form of the common prayer.

**Bid (German).** Geliebten im herrn Christo, weil wir im namen unsers herren Christi jetzt

**Bid (English).** Beloved in the Lord Christ, forasmuch as we are now here gathered together in
the name of our Lord Christ, and have God’s commandment and bidding that we should pray, and
moreover the promise that whatsoever we ask in the name of Jesus Christ, our gracious Father in

### D.2 Thanksgiving

*Witnesses:* Solms-Laubach 1576.

**Rubric (English).** At the end of the sermon the matters handled shall be briefly repeated,

**Bid (German).** Dieweil wir nun Gottes wortt haben angehort und schuldig seint, Gott dem

what pertaineth thereto; and we have God’s promise and sure assurance that whatsoever we shall
ask in the name of Jesus Christ, the Father in heaven will give it us.

**Bid (English).** Ye elect, pray God the Father of all mercy, through Jesus Christ our dear

**Bid (English).** Let us therefore first consider the need of all Christendom, and pray that

### D.5 Peace

*Witnesses:* Erbach 1560, Solms-Laubach 1576 and Henneberg 1582.

**Bid (German).** Darnach last uns auch bitten für dieses zeitlich leben, daß uns Gott mit
gnedigem fride und seligem Wetter begnaden, vor Theurung, Kriege unnd Pestilentz behüten wölle.

**Bid (English).** Thereafter let us also pray for this temporal life, that God would bless us
with gracious peace and seasonable weather, and keep us from dearth, war and pestilence.

### D.6 Civil authority

*Witnesses:* Erbach 1560, Solms-Laubach 1576 and Henneberg 1582.

**Bid (German).** Bittet auch für alle Oberkeit, Keyser, König, Fürsten und Herrn, In sonderheit

**Bid (English).** Pray ye also for all magistrates, Emperor, kings, princes and lords,

and word, that God’s word and honour may be furthered and not hindered by them, and that we also
may have the more peace under their government.

### D.7 All estates / households

*Witnesses:* Nürnberg 1545 and Waldeck 1556.

**Bid (German).** Ferner bittet für alle eheleute, hausherrn und frauen, arbeiter, handierer,

so in anfechtung stecken, betrübte, irrige, verfürte gewissen oder was dergleichen sein. Der

**Bid (English).** Further, pray ye for all married folk, masters and mistresses of households,
labourers, tradesmen, servants, sinners men and women, the sick, the captives, the sorrowful,
forsaken widows and orphans, and them that are in temptation, sorrowful, erring and misled
consciences, or whatsoever else there be of the like. The eternal, gracious God have mercy upon
all, pardon their misdeeds, vouchsafe them an honourable, peaceable, honest conversation
according to his word, and let them sink in no temptation, but graciously deliver them from all
evil, be it bodily or ghostly, through pure doctrine and firm faith. Amen.

**Bid (English).** Forasmuch also as here and there in all lands many poor Christians are for

### D.9 The afflicted

*Witnesses:* Erbach 1560, Solms-Laubach 1576 and Henneberg 1582.

**Bid (German).** Darnach last uns auch bitten für unsere arme Brüder und schwestern, hie unnd

graciously deliver them.

## 9. Family E: Saxon Gemein Gebet (Melanchthon/Pfeffinger)

The Albertine Saxon "Gemein Gebet" in the Melanchthon/Pfeffinger tradition, a continuous prayer
with pronounced petitions against war and for the Elector.

- **Earliest witness (archetype):** Leipzig 1567.
- **Tradition:** Lutheran.
- **Language:** German.
- **Form:** continuous prayer.
- **Place in the service (Leipzig 1567):** After the sermon, “mit mund und herzen zu sprechen”.

| Year | Witness | Territory | Sehling | Form |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1567 | Leipzig, Gemein gebet (renewed by Johann Pfeffinger) | Leipzig (Electoral Saxony) | Sehling 1, IV. Theil, Nr. 108, pp. 595–596 | continuous prayer |

**Sequence:** E.1 Repentance & forgiveness > E.2 The Church > E.3 Civil authority > E.4 Peace >
E.5 Civil authority > E.6 The afflicted.

### E.1 Repentance & forgiveness

**Rubric (German).** Gemein gebet. Nach der predigt mit mund und herzen zu sprechen.

**Rubric (English).** Common prayer. To be said after the sermon with mouth and heart.

**Prayer (German).** Almechtiger got und vater unsers herrn Jesu Christi, wir bekennen, das wir
mit vielen sünden, wissentlich und unwissentlich vorunreinet sind, und ist uns herzlich leid,
das wir wider deinen gerechten willen gethan haben, und bitten, du wollest unsere herzen zu dir
bekeren, und uns alle unsere sünde, umb deines lieben sohns Jesu Christi willen vorgeben, und
wollest in uns mit deinem heiligen geist rechten glauben und rechten gehorsam anzünden, und uns
regieren. So ist auch unser ernstlicher fürsatz, uns mit deiner hülf zu bessern, und in deinem
gehorsam zu leben.

**Prayer (English).** Almighty God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, we confess that we are
defiled with many sins, wittingly and unwittingly, and it grieveth us heartily that we have done
against thy righteous will; and we beseech thee that thou wouldest turn our hearts unto thee,
and forgive us all our sins for thy dear Son Jesus Christ’s sake, and kindle in us by thy Holy
Spirit right faith and right obedience, and govern us. So is it also our earnest purpose to
amend ourselves with thy help and to live in thy obedience.

### E.2 The Church

**Prayer (German).** Wir bitten auch, umb deins lieben sohns, Jesu Christi willen, du wollest
unter uns in diesen landen für und für eine kirche samlen, und christlich reine lehre erhalten,
treue lehrer und prediger geben, die mit rechter lehr und gutem exempel, zu deinem lob und der
leute seligkeit dienen.

**Prayer (English).** We beseech thee also, for thy dear Son Jesus Christ’s sake, that thou
wouldest evermore gather a Church among us in these lands, and preserve Christian pure doctrine,
and give faithful teachers and preachers, which with right doctrine and good example may serve
to thy praise and the salvation of the people.

### E.3 Civil authority

**Prayer (German).** Und bitten von herzen, du wollest umb deines lieben sohns Jesu Christi
willen, disen landen fridlich christlich selig regiment geben, und römische keiserliche majestät
und unsern herrschaften herzog regenten churfursten und seiner churfürstlichen gnaden zugethane
und verwante, mit dem heiligen geist regieren, das ir leben und regierung, dir zu lobe, und inen
und den unterthanen zu frieden und seligkeit dienen möge.

**Prayer (English).** And we beseech thee from our heart that thou wouldest, for thy dear Son
Jesus Christ’s sake, give unto these lands peaceable, Christian, blessed government, and govern
with the Holy Spirit the Roman Imperial Majesty and our lordships, the duke regent Elector and
his Electoral Grace’s adherents and kinsfolk, that their life and government may serve to thy
praise, and to peace and salvation for them and for the subjects.

### E.4 Peace

**Prayer (German).** Wollest auch ire churfürstlichen gnaden sampt derselbigen zugethanen und

barmherzigkeit, behüten, schützen und bewaren. Die widerwertigen, unruigen und ungehorsamen, so
göttlicher ordnung widerstreben, die herrschaft vorachten, den frieden brechen, und zu
blutvergiessen, leut und land vorderbung ursach geben, gnediglich bekeren, oder ihnen steuren
und wehren, und den befolnen rechtmessigen kriege, mit seliger wolfart zu gutem, glücklichem,
friedlichem ende bringen, auf das vieler christen unschuldiges blutvergiessen, vorwüstung dieser
landen, schulen und kirchen, abgewendet und vorhutet werden.

**Prayer (English).** Wilt thou also by thy bottomless mercy guard, protect and preserve his
Electoral Grace, together with his adherents and kinsmen, in these grievous and other matters of
war; graciously convert the adversaries, the unquiet and disobedient, which resist the divine
ordinance, despise authority, break the peace, and give occasion to bloodshed and the ruin of
people and land, or else restrain and withstand them; and bring the lawful war committed unto us
to a good, happy, peaceable end with blessed welfare, that the innocent bloodshed of many
Christians and the wasting of these lands, schools and churches may be turned away and
prevented.

### E.5 Civil authority

**Prayer (German).** Und wollest auch dieser stadt obrigkeit und unterthanen gnediglich regieren
und bewahren, und die früchte der erden (die du giebst, das in diesem leiblichen leben die

geben und segnen.

**Prayer (English).** And wilt thou also graciously govern and preserve the magistrates and
subjects of this city, and willingly give and bless the fruits of the earth (which thou givest
that men may be preserved in this bodily life, to learn to know thee and call upon thee) and our
daily bread.

### E.6 The afflicted

**Prayer (German).** Du weist auch die grosse schwachheit menschlicher natur, und wilt darumb
deinen grossen zorn mit barmherzigkeit lindern, von wegen des vordiensts und fürbits deines
allerliebsten sohns Jesu Christi unsers herren willen. Darumb bitten wir dich, du wollest unser
wolverdienten strafen gnediglich lindern, und alle betrübten, die dich anrufen, mit deinem
heiligen geist trösten, das sie in rechtem glauben und rechter anrufung bleiben, und deine
barmherzigkeit in ewigkeit preisen. Amen.

**Prayer (English).** Thou knowest also the great weakness of man’s nature, and wilt therefore
temper thy great wrath with mercy, for the sake of the merit and intercession of thy most dear
Son Jesus Christ our Lord. Therefore we beseech thee that thou wouldest graciously temper our
well-deserved punishments, and comfort with thy Holy Spirit all the sorrowful that call upon
thee, that they may abide in right faith and right calling upon thee, and praise thy mercy for
ever. Amen.

## 10. Family F: Upper German pulpit intercession (Strasbourg/Zürich)

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1537 | Augsburg, Kirchenordnung (Die zehen gebot, Articul des Glaubens, Und das Vater unser) (Moderate Reformed) | Augsburg (Imperial city) | Sehling 12, I.8a, pp. 70–71 | prayer with rubrics + Lord’s Prayer |

### F.1 Exhortation / preface

**Rubric (German).** Fürbitt für die gemeinen stend:

**Rubric (English).** Intercession for the common estates:

**Bid (German).** Dieweil es aber vor Gott unserm hailand gut und angenäm ist, und er uns durch
seinen hailigen apostel bevolhen hat, für alle menschen zu bitten, so lassend uns in seinem
namen, in dem uns alles guts verhaißen ist, mit warem glauben und herzlichem vertrauen für Gott
unsern gütigen Vater treten und also sprechen:

**Bid (English).** But forasmuch as it is good and acceptable before God our Saviour, and he
hath commanded us by his holy Apostle to pray for all men, let us in his name, wherein all good

### F.2 All men

**Prayer (German).** Allmechtiger ewiger Gott, lieber getreuer Vater im himel, wie sich dein
große gnad und gütigkeit reichlich erzaigt gegen allen menschen, also, dieweil wir deine kinder

**Prayer (English).** Almighty, everlasting God, dear and faithful Father in heaven: as thy
great grace and goodness showeth itself richly toward all men, so, forasmuch as we are thy
children, we desire from our hearts to take after thy fatherly bounty as far as may be, and to
show forth our love toward all men in Christian wise by our faithful intercession, since we can
do no more; and we call upon thee therefore, through thy Son Jesus Christ, our dear Lord and
Saviour, for all the estates and concerns of thy holy Christendom.

### F.3 Civil authority

**Prayer (German).** Erstlich für unsern herr kaiser, künig, all fürsten herren und regenten,
sonderlich aber für unser ersame oberkait in diser statt. Verleich inen allen den gaist deiner

**Prayer (English).** First for our lord the Emperor, the King, all princes, lords and rulers,
but especially for our worshipful magistrates in this city. Vouchsafe unto them all the spirit
of thy fear and wisdom, that they may order all their government to the praise and glory of thy
great name and to the peaceable preservation of the subjects in all honesty and godliness. But
unto the subjects give a willing and seemly heart to obey the magistrates in all that is not
against thee.

**Prayer (German).** Auch bitten wir dich von herzen, verleich allen dienern und verkündigern
deines hailgen worts, das si mit gesunder lere und unsträflichem leben deiner hailgen gmaind zu
täglicher besserung christelich vorstandend. Sonderlich aber, getreuer Vater, wöllest alle gute
mittel jederzeit gnädiglich fügen, damit weder uns noch unsern nachkomen an getreuen ausspendern
deiner gehaimnus und des trostlichen evangelii von deinem Sun Jesu Christo nimmer mehr mangle!

**Prayer (English).** We beseech thee also from our hearts, grant unto all ministers and
preachers of thy holy word that with sound doctrine and unblameable life they may preside in
Christian wise over thy holy congregation to its daily amendment. But especially, faithful
Father, ordain graciously at all times all good means, that neither we nor our posterity may
ever lack faithful stewards of thy mysteries and of the comfortable Gospel of thy Son Jesus
Christ.

### F.5 All estates / households

**Prayer (German).** Erbarm dich auch, gnediger Gott, über alle hausväter und hausmütern, auch
schulmaister und alle die, so kind oder gesind haben, das si dieselbigen gotseliglich nach
anweisung deines worts zu deinem preis und nutz deiner kirchen erziehen und regieren! Laß auch
lieber Got, deinen frid und segen reichlich walten über all ehleut, damit si in lieb und
ainigkait bei ainander wonen und all ehlich beschwerden mit glauben und gedult tragen mögen!
Sonderlich aber verleich solchs disen personen, die sich begeren, ehlich mitainander zu
verpflichten, mit namen etc.

**Prayer (English).** Have mercy also, gracious God, upon all fathers and mothers of households,
and also schoolmasters and all that have children or servants, that they may bring them up and
govern them godly according to the direction of thy word, to thy praise and the profit of thy
Church. Let also, dear God, thy peace and blessing prevail richly over all married folk, that
they may dwell together in love and unity and bear all the burdens of wedlock with faith and
patience. But especially vouchsafe this unto these persons that desire to bind themselves
together in marriage, by name, etc.

### F.6 Special intercessions

**Rubric (German).** Hie sollen die hochzeiten verkünd werden und dann darauf weiter also
gesprochen:

**Rubric (English).** Here the marriages shall be published, and then thereupon further said
thus:

**Prayer (German).** Verleich in, barmherziger Vater, disen hailgen stand in deiner forcht
anzetreten, das si auch christelich darin leben und denselbigen seliglich beschließen mögen!

**Prayer (English).** Grant them, merciful Father, to enter upon this holy estate in thy fear,
that they may also live therein in Christian wise and close the same blessedly.

### F.7 Women with child

**Prayer (German).** Wöllest auch, o himlischer Vater, die geperenden frauen mit gnaden empinden
und si mit ainer wolgetonen und dir gefelligen frucht gnediglich erfröuen!

**Prayer (English).** Wilt thou also, O heavenly Father, graciously deliver women in travail and
gladden them graciously with a well-formed fruit, well-pleasing unto thee.

### F.8 Schools & youth

**Prayer (German).** Auch bitten wir dich, gütiger Vater, für unser unerzogne jugent, das du si
durch deinen Hailigen Gaist väterlich aufziehen und nach deinem willen und wolgefallen bilden
wöllest, damit si in deiner forcht und glauben an deinen Sun Jesum Christum seliglich aufwachsen

**Prayer (English).** We pray thee also, gracious Father, for our youth not yet brought up, that
thou wouldest bring them up in fatherly wise by thy Holy Spirit and form them according to thy
will and good pleasure, that they may grow up blessedly in thy fear and in faith in thy Son
Jesus Christ, and that every service of thy congregation may evermore be well and christianly
supplied by them.

### F.9 All estates / households

**Prayer (German).** Gib dein gnedigs gedeihen und benedeiung allen getreuen eehalten und
arbaitern! Die armen begab mit leibsnarung und christlicher glassenhait, die reichen mit
gläubiger miltigkait!

**Prayer (English).** Give thy gracious increase and blessing to all faithful servants and
labourers. Endow the poor with bodily sustenance and Christian resignation, the rich with
faithful liberality.

### F.10 The afflicted

**Prayer (German).** Erzaig dich ain getreuen milten Vater allen betrübten, angefochtnen herzen
von innen oder außen! Den kranken verleich langmütig gedultigkait, den sterbenden ain fröliche

**Prayer (English).** Show thyself a faithful and bountiful Father to all sorrowful hearts,
tempted within or without. Grant unto the sick long-suffering patience, to the dying a joyful
going up to heaven. And in especial have mercy upon certain which at this time in their
suffering, sickness and tribulation desire our intercession.

### F.11 Special intercessions

**Rubric (German).** Hie soll er die verkündigen, so des gemainen bets begeren, und darauf dann

**Rubric (English).** Here he shall announce them that desire the common prayer, and thereupon

**Prayer (English).** Let all that befall them in fatherly wise, faithful God, which according

### F.12 The persecuted

**Prayer (German).** Erhalt mit deiner gewaltigen hand alle, die umb deines names und der

**Prayer (English).** Uphold with thy mighty hand all that for thy name’s and for righteousness’

### F.13 The erring & unbelievers

**Prayer (German).** Weis und lait alle irrige den weg christlicher warhait und des ewigen

christlichen glauben und ainigkait deiner kirchen! Treib aus den unglauben aus aller menschen
herzen, damit dein reich täglich je mehr und mehr zukomme und dein aingeborner Sun, unser lieber

**Prayer (English).** Show and lead all that err into the way of Christian truth and of eternal

and Saviour Jesus Christ, be known to the whole world and be lauded and praised of it.

**Rubric (German).** Wider den türken und die verfolger des evangelions

**Rubric (English).** Against the Turk and the persecutors of the Gospel

**Bid (German).** Schliest auch darneben ein den greulichen erbfeind des christlichen namens,

alle die, für welche wir schuldig seind zu bitten, wöllen wir mit herzlicher demütigkait nit
allain mit dem mund, sonder von ganzem herzen zu Gott bitten und also sprechen: Unser vater etc.

**Bid (English).** Include also therewith the horrible hereditary enemy of the Christian name,
the Turk; praying that almighty God would smite him with the breath of his Holy Spirit, that the
name of Jesus Christ be not blasphemed by him, nor so much Christian blood shed by him. For this
and all other need of the whole of Christendom, and also for ourselves and for all for whom we
are bound to pray, we will pray to God with hearty humility, not with the mouth only but with
the whole heart, and say thus: Our Father, etc.

### F.15 Against the Turk

**Rubric (German).** Oder also:

**Rubric (English).** Or thus:

**Prayer (German).** Du wöllest auch, o getreuer Gott und Vater, dem gewalt des tyrannischen,
widerchristischen reichs des Türken, so wider Christum frävenlich setzt, sampt aller abgötterei,

stürzen, damit nit dein götlicher nam sampt dem glauben Christi in uns von dem unglaubigen volk
und feinden der warheit verspottet und geschmächt, sonder durch deinen Hailigen Gaist erweitert

**Prayer (English).** Wilt thou also, O faithful God and Father, with thine almighty power

wantonly against Christ, together with all idolatry, tyranny and ungodly being, and cast it down
to be the footstool of Christ’s feet; that thy divine name, together with the faith of Christ in
us, be not mocked and reviled by the unbelieving people and the enemies of the truth, but be
enlarged and spread abroad by thy Holy Spirit, and the enemy be driven back through Christ
Jesus.

### F.16 Lord's Prayer

**Rubric (German).** Volget das Vater unser:

**Rubric (English).** The Our Father followeth:

**Bid (German).** In desselbigen namen, o treuer, gütiger und barmherziger Vater, und aus seinem
gehaiß komen wir für dein angesicht und rufen dich an, wie uns unser Herr Jesus Christus selbs
geleert und zu beten bevolhen hat, und sprechend:

**Bid (English).** In his name, O faithful, gracious and merciful Father, and at his bidding, we
come before thy face and call upon thee, as our Lord Jesus Christ himself hath taught and
commanded us to pray, and say:

**Prayer (German).** Unser Vater, der du bist in den himlen. Gehailiget werd dein nam! Zukumme
dein reich! Dein will geschech auf erden wie im himel! Unser täglich brot gib uns heut und
vergib uns unsere schuld, als und wir vergeben unsern schuldigern, und für uns nit in
versuchung, sonder erlös uns von dem bösen! Dann dein ist das reich und die kraft und die
herrlichait in ewigkait. Amen.

**Prayer (English).** Our Father, which art in the heavens. Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom
come. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread, and
forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us
from evil: for thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen.

### G.1 Exhortation / preface

**Rubric (German).** Es mag aber der diener des worts die ganze kirche zum gebet uf diese weise

**Rubric (English).** But the minister of the word may exhort the whole church to prayer on this
wise: (in the margin: Exhortation to prayer)

**Bid (German).** Ihr geliebten im Herrn, es ist kein mensch, der nit wisse und verstehe, sobald
er sein eigen gewissen fragt, daß er hulf, woltat, gnade und barmherzigkeit Gottes zu allen
zeiten und allenthalben bedarf, dann wir seind allesampt arme, schwache und gebrechliche
menschen. Wir seind aber gelehret vom Herrn Christo, daß wir alles guts, ja was uns nütz und not

schuldig nach dem befelch und exempel Christi, auch vor andere zu bitten und nit allein vor die,
so uns guts günnen und tun, sondern auch vor alle stende der menschen, ja auch vor unsere feinde
und verfolger, darzü auch fur wasserlei not. Und wie wir zu Gott söllen unser gebet tun
insgemein, werden alle gottfürchtigen von dem Geist Gottes unterrichtet. Uber das alles haben
wir ein gebott von Christo, daß wir von seinem himlischen Vatter, der da ist und will erkant
sein von allen menschen als ein gütiger Herr und warer almechtiger Gott und Vatter, der uns
leichtlich könne und auch wölle gnediglich geben, alles was nutz und heilsam ist, in seinem
namen bestendiglich begeren sollen. Es hat uns auch unser Herr Christus verheißen, was wir den
himlischen Vatter in seinem namen bitten werden, das will er uns willig und reichlich geben.
Zuletzt wissen wir auch aus der verheißung Christi, daß eines iglichen gleubigen gebet, ich
geschweige der ganzen gemein, im namen Christi ordentlich versamlet, soll erhöret werden umb
Christi willen. Daraus volget unwidersprechlich, daß das gemein gebet ein sonderlich merkzeichen
und ubung sei der christlichen kirchen und aller gleubigen, welchs dem almechtigen Gott
wolgefellig und angenem sei. Derhalben auch wir in dieser unser versamlung mit einmütigem herzen
im Geist und in der warheit Gott den Herren nit allein fur uns, sonder auch fur die ganze kirche
und alle stende und noturft der menschen anruffen.

**Bid (English).** Dearly beloved in the Lord, there is no man but knoweth and understandeth, so
soon as he asketh his own conscience, that he needeth the help, benefits, grace and mercy of God
at all times and in all places; for we are all of us poor, weak and frail men. But we are taught
of the Lord Christ that we should ask and look for all good, yea, whatsoever is profitable and
needful for us, from our heavenly Father alone, who ever knoweth what we need, with an humble
and penitent heart out of true faith. Moreover, if we will witness that there is in us a spark
of brotherly and unfeigned love, we are bound by the commandment and example of Christ to pray
for others also, and not only for them that wish us well and do us good, but also for all
estates of men, yea, also for our enemies and persecutors, and for whatsoever need besides. And
how we should make our prayer unto God in common, all the God-fearing are instructed by the
Spirit of God. Over and above all this we have a commandment from Christ that we should
steadfastly ask in his name of his heavenly Father, who is, and will be known of all men as a
gracious Lord and true almighty God and Father, who easily can and also will graciously give us
all that is profitable and wholesome. Our Lord Christ hath also promised us that whatsoever we
shall ask the heavenly Father in his name, he will give it us willingly and richly. Lastly, we
know also from the promise of Christ that the prayer of every believer, to say nothing of the
whole congregation lawfully gathered in the name of Christ, shall be heard for Christ’s sake.
Whence it followeth undeniably that the common prayer is a special mark and exercise of the
Christian Church and of all believers, which is well-pleasing and acceptable unto almighty God.
Therefore we also in this our assembly, with one mind in the Spirit and in truth, call upon God
the Lord not only for ourselves but also for the whole Church and all estates and necessities of
men:

**Bid (English).** First and before all things for all ministers of God’s word and for the happy
course of the Gospel, that the same may be planted and spread abroad in all lands, and so the
number of the believers in Christ may grow and increase, as we have commandment thereof from
Christ and the Apostle Paul: namely, that utterance may be given unto the preachers to proclaim
the word with the joyful opening of their mouth, that the mystery of the Gospel of Christ may be
made known and manifest, and that they may deal and speak boldly therein, as it becometh, to the
furtherance and joy of the faith of the congregation; and that unto the Church and congregation
the grace in the Holy Ghost may be given in Christ, that she may be enriched in all doctrine and
all knowledge, as the preaching of Christ becometh mighty in her, so that she come behind in no
gift, and walk unblameably and worthily of the Gospel of Christ, and so stand fast in one spirit
and in one soul, striving for the faith of the Gospel, and be in nothing terrified by the
adversaries, but hold fast to the Gospel unto the end and wait for the revelation of Jesus
Christ.

**Bid (German).** Zum andern wollen wir auch bitten fur keiser, könige, fürsten, obrigkeit und

**Bid (English).** Secondly, we will also pray for the Emperor, kings, princes, magistrates and

### G.4 The erring & unbelievers

**Bid (German).** Zum dritten fur alle diejenigen, so verlangen haben zur erkentnus der warheit,

**Bid (English).** Thirdly, for all them that long for the knowledge of the truth, be they who
they will; and also them that in foreign lands are persecuted and martyred for the truth’s sake.

### G.5 Enemies

**Bid (German).** Zum vierten wollen wir auch bitten fur alle unsere widersacher und verfolger,
vornemlich aber fur diejenigen, so aus unwissenheit der warheit widerstreben und die christen
unbillicherweise verfolgen, daß ihnen Gott der Herr dies gnediglich vergebe und diese schwere

**Bid (English).** Fourthly, we will also pray for all our adversaries and persecutors, but
chiefly for them that out of ignorance resist the truth and persecute the Christians unjustly,
that God the Lord would graciously forgive them this and not impute this grievous sin unto them,
and that he would lead such to the knowledge of the truth, that they also may be saved.

### G.6 The afflicted

**Bid (German).** Zum fünften fur alle kranken und diejenigen, so ein innerlich oder eußerlich
anfechtung haben, auch fur alle, so von wegen des kreuzes, welches ihnen Gott zuschickt, betrübt
sind, daß sie mögen getröstet werden, wie wir lesen in der epistel Iacobi cap. 5.

**Bid (English).** Fifthly, for all the sick and them that have an inward or outward temptation,

be comforted, as we read in the Epistle of James, chapter 5.

### G.7 Deliverance from calamities

**Bid (German).** Zum sechsten wollen wir insonderheit auch Gott anruffen fur gegenwertige

**Bid (English).** Sixthly, we will also call upon God especially for the present common
necessity. This petition is needful when war, hunger or dearth, or else other need and mishaps
are at hand.

### G.8 Conclusion

**Bid (German).** Zum siebenden laßt uns auch bitten fur alle dasjenige, so der ganzen
christlichen gemeine angelegen ist, fur alles, darumb Gott will angeruffen sein. Wir wollen auch

**Bid (English).** Seventhly, let us also pray for all that concerneth the whole Christian
congregation, for all for which God will be called upon. We will also pray one for another, for
ourselves that are here come together. And let us first forgive one another, and thereafter call
upon God and pray for grace and forgiveness of sins.

### G.9 Special intercessions

**Rubric (German).** Diese form des gebets mag man kürzer oder lenger machen, nach einer
jeglichen kirchen gelegenheit.

**Rubric (English).** This form of prayer may be made shorter or longer, according to the
circumstances of each church.

**Sequence:** I.1 Exhortation / preface > I.2 Civil authority > I.3 The persecuted > I.4 Peace >

### I.1 Exhortation / preface

**Rubric (German).** Eine kurtze vermanung und vorbitt, für die christlichen stend zu bitten

**Rubric (English).** A short exhortation and intercession, to pray for the Christian estates

**Bid (German).** Dieweil wir nun jetzunder allhie beyeinander versammlet seind nach Gottes
bevelch, sein heylsams wort zu hören und volgents auch unser gebett zu tun zu Gott dem Herren,
in dem namen Christi umb allerley anligende not der gantzen christenheyt, so wöllen wir Gott den
Allmechtigen, unsern himmlischen vatter, auß kindlicher zuversicht mit glaubigem hertzen
anruffen und bitten, das er uns gnediglich erhören und geweren wölle, von wegen seiner götlichen
warheit und verheyssung, so uns durch seinen lieben son zugesagt ist worden, das uns Got der
Herr erstlich und fürnemlich sein heyligs wort, das gnadenreyche evangelion auß vätterlicher
trew wölle mitteilen, verleyhen, schützen und erhalten, auf das wir dasselbig reyn und lauter
mit rechtem verstand mögen verkündigen, hören und folg tun, das dardurch sein nam unter uns und
in aller welt geheyliget und gepreyset werde. Gott der Herr wölle auch alle falsche lehr, irrtum
und ketzerey außreuten und uns gnediglich darvor behütten, auf das sein reich zu uns komme und
uns regiere mit seinem wort, gnaden und Heiligen Geist, dardurch des Satans, Machomets und
Antichristens reich ein ende nemme, verheret und verstöret werde. Damit also sein vätterlicher
will geschehe, nit des fleysches, der welt und des Satans will, sondern sein will geschehe hie
unter uns auf erden wie droben im hymmel. Darzu wölle Gott der Allmechtig allenthalben
verordnen, erhalten, schützen und behüten alle trewe bischof, pfarherrn, prediger und
kirchendiener.

**Bid (English).** Forasmuch as we are now here gathered together according to God’s
commandment, to hear his wholesome word and thereafter also to make our prayer unto God the Lord
in the name of Christ for all manner of pressing need of the whole of Christendom, we will call
upon God the Almighty, our heavenly Father, out of childlike confidence with believing hearts,
and pray him that he would graciously hear and grant us, for the sake of his divine truth and
promise which hath been made us by his dear Son: that God the Lord would first and chiefly, out
of fatherly faithfulness, impart, vouchsafe, defend and preserve unto us his holy word, the
gracious Gospel, that we may preach, hear and obey the same purely and clearly with right
understanding, that thereby his name may be hallowed and praised among us and in all the world.
That God the Lord would also root out all false doctrine, error and heresy, and graciously keep
us therefrom, that his kingdom may come to us and rule us with his word, grace and Holy Spirit,
whereby the kingdom of Satan, of Mahomet and of Antichrist may come to an end and be laid waste
and destroyed; that so his fatherly will may be done, not the will of the flesh, the world and
Satan, but his will be done here among us on earth as above in heaven. Moreover, that God
Almighty would everywhere ordain, preserve, defend and guard all faithful bishops, pastors,
preachers and ministers of the Church.

### I.2 Civil authority

**Bid (German).** Wir wöllen auch bitten für die christlichen oberkeyt, für Keyserliche und
Königliche Mayestet, für alle churfürsten, fürsten, herren und alle stend des Reychs. In
sonderheyt aber lasset uns bitten für unsere schutzherrn und potentaten, deßgleychen auch für
ihre rät, amptleut und diener. Gott der Herr wölle ihnen allen miteinander geben ware forcht
Gottes, weyßheyt und verstand, seliglich und wol zu regieren, auf das wir unter ihrem regiment
mögen noch lenger haben Gottes wort, zeytlichen fried und narung.

**Bid (English).** We will also pray for the Christian magistrates, for the Imperial and Royal
Majesty, for all Electors, princes, lords and all the estates of the Empire. But especially let
us pray for our protecting lords and potentates, and likewise for their counsellors, officers
and servants. God the Lord give them all together the true fear of God, wisdom and understanding
to rule blessedly and well, that under their government we may yet longer have God’s word,
temporal peace and sustenance.

### I.3 The persecuted

**Bid (German).** Wir wöllen auch Gott in sonderheyt ferner anrüffen und bitten, das er die
werden christenheit, so allenthalben betrengt, geenstigt und verfolgt wird von den unglaubigen,
dem Türken, machometischen, widerchristischen, blutdürstigen leuten, das Got die armen christen
gnediglich schützen, retten und im rechten glauben erhalten wölle.

**Bid (English).** We will also call upon God especially further and pray him for worthy
Christendom, which everywhere is oppressed, afflicted and persecuted by the unbelievers, the
Turk, Mahometan, antichristian, bloodthirsty people: that God would graciously defend and
deliver the poor Christians and keep them in the right faith.

### I.4 Peace

**Bid (German).** Lasset uns auch bitten für alle christliche untertanen, das sie Gott der Herr
in schuldiger pflicht und gehorsam gnediglich erhalten wölle und sie behüten vor zwitracht,
aufruhr und uns alle also vätterlich schützen und bewaren vor krieg, blutvergiessen, teurung,
seuche, pestilentz, hagel, schaur, schedlichem ungewitter, vor allem unziffer, vergiftem luft
und dergleichen, auf das wir unser teglich brot in aller stille, fried, ruhe und gesundheyt
bekommen mögen.

**Bid (English).** Let us also pray for all Christian subjects, that God the Lord would
graciously keep them in due duty and obedience and guard them from discord and uproar, and so
defend and preserve us all in fatherly wise from war, bloodshed, dearth, sickness, pestilence,
hail, storm, hurtful tempest, from all vermin, poisoned air and the like, that we may receive
our daily bread in all stillness, peace, rest and health.

### I.5 Repentance & forgiveness

**Bid (German).** Lasset uns auch bitten, das uns Gott der Herr unsere schuld gnediglich
verzeihen wölle, verschonen unsers gebrechlichen lebens, die alten und vorigen sünd nicht
zurechnen, sondern auß gnaden verzeihen in Christo unserm herrn. Wölle uns auch ein solch hertz
geben, das wir auß rechtem grund können verzeihen unsern mißgünnern und feinden, damit wir ein
liebreich hertz haben gegen unserm nehesten. Gott der Herr wölle uns auch nit in anfechtung
füren und nit in versuchung fallen lassen, sondern uns auß gnaden erhalten, damit wir nit in die
schedlichen versuchung und anreitzung fallen des fleisches, der welt und des Satans. Sonder Gott
der Almechtig wölle uns erlösen von dem bösen, von allem ubel leibs und der seelen, zeitlichem
und ewigen schaden.

**Bid (English).** Let us also pray that God the Lord would graciously forgive us our debt,
spare our frail life, not impute the old and former sins, but forgive them of grace in Christ
our Lord; that he would also give us such an heart that we can forgive from the right ground
them that grudge against us and our enemies, that we may have a loving heart toward our
neighbour. That God the Lord would also not lead us into temptation nor let us fall into trial,
but keep us of grace, that we fall not into the hurtful temptation and enticement of the flesh,
the world and Satan; but that God Almighty would deliver us from evil, from all harm of body and
soul, temporal and eternal.

### I.6 Women with child

**Bid (German).** In sonderheit lasset uns bitten für alle schwangere und geberende frawen, für
witwen und waysen, für die liebe jugent, für die ehleut, sonderlich wo der böse feind seinen
samen geseet hat, zwitracht, verbitterung und uneinigkeit, das sich auch die nachbaurn einig,
fridlich, gutwillig, dienstlich und liebreich gegeneinander erzeigen.

**Bid (English).** In especial let us pray for all women with child and in travail, for widows
and orphans, for the dear youth, for married folk, especially where the evil enemy hath sown his
seed, discord, bitterness and disunity; that neighbours also may show themselves one toward
another united, peaceable, well-willing, serviceable and loving.

### I.7 Special intercessions

**Bid (German).** Bittet zum beschluß für etliche kranke, angefochtne, kleinmütige und trostlose
personen, welche sich in sonderheit in ewer christlich gebett trewlich befelhen. Bittet auch für
die, für welche wir sonderlich schuldig seind zu bitten, so uns in unser ampt und gebett
befolhen sein.

**Bid (English).** Pray ye in conclusion for certain sick, tempted, fainthearted and comfortless
persons, which commend themselves faithfully in especial to your Christian prayer. Pray ye also
for them for whom we are especially bound to pray, which are commended to us in our office and
prayer.

### I.8 Lord's Prayer

**Bid (German).** Für dise und alle andere not der gantzen christenheyt, auch für uns selber und
die unsern, wöllen wir Gott den Herrn von hertzen anruffen. Darumb tut ewer gebet zu Gott dem
Almechtigen in dem namen unsers Herrn von gantzem hertzen und bettet mit andacht inniglich von
hertzen ein Vatter unser.

**Bid (English).** For this and all other need of the whole of Christendom, and also for
ourselves and ours, we will call upon God the Lord from the heart. Therefore make your prayer to
God Almighty in the name of our Lord with your whole heart, and pray with devotion, inwardly,
from the heart, an Our Father.

### I.9 Conclusion

**Rubric (German).** Nach dem gebet der beschluß:

**Rubric (English).** After the prayer, the conclusion:

**Prayer (German).** Der allmechtige Gott hat unser gebett nach seiner göttlichen verheissung
gnediglich erhöret in Christo, unserm herrn. Dem sey lob, ehr, ruhm und preiß immer und

**Prayer (English).** Almighty God hath graciously heard our prayer according to his divine
promise, in Christ our Lord. To him be laud, honour, glory and praise, now and for ever. Amen.
Let the poor and needy be faithfully commended to you in your almsgiving.

**Rubric (German).** Darauf sol das volk abermal zum gebet und danksagung gegen Gott vermanet
werden und samptlich vor erhaltung der kirchen Gottes und rechter lere, darzu der treuen
warhaftigen lerer und das Gott treue arbeiter in seine ernte senden wolle, für die oberkeit, für

sonderbarer personen, die des christlichen gebets begeren, wie des ungefehrlich eine notel

**Rubric (English).** Thereupon the people shall again be exhorted to prayer and thanksgiving
toward God, and all together for the preservation of the Church of God and of right doctrine,
and moreover of the faithful, true teachers, and that God would send faithful labourers into his
harvest; for the magistrates, for temporal peace and the growth of the fruits; and in sum for
the need of the whole of Christendom and of particular persons that desire the Christian prayer;
whereof a draft followeth, after this manner.

**Bid (German).** Lieben Christen, last uns Gott bitten, das er uns bey seinem göttlichen wort
wolle gnediglich erhalten und getreue arbeiter in seine ernte senden, den predigern und
kirchendienern seinen heiligen Geist verleihen, das sie dasselbige rein und recht fürtragen und
leren, auch mit gottseligem leben fürgehen und das der allmechtige wolle sie und uns vor
falscher lere gnediglich behüten und derselbigen steuren und wehren. Das er auch unsern brüdern
und schwestern, die mit falscher lere oder sonst mit unrechter gewalt von unchristen oder andern
tyrannen angefochten und beschwert werden, gnediglich helfen wolle, das sie in warem glauben und
gedult bestendig bleiben, und so möglich, die verfolgung von ihnen genomen oder gemiltert werde.

**Bid (English).** Dear Christians, let us pray God that he would graciously keep us in his
divine word, and send faithful labourers into his harvest; vouchsafe his Holy Spirit unto the
preachers and ministers of the Church, that they may set forth and teach the same purely and
rightly, and also go before with godly life; and that the Almighty would graciously keep them
and us from false doctrine, and restrain and withstand the same. That he would also graciously
help our brethren and sisters which are assailed and oppressed with false doctrine or else with
unrighteous violence by unchristian men or other tyrants, that they may abide steadfast in true
faith and patience, and, if it be possible, that the persecution may be taken from them or
mitigated.

### J.2 Civil authority

*Witnesses:* Lüneburg 1564, Braunschweig-Wolfenbüttel 1569 and Oldenburg 1573.

**Bid (German).** Bittet auch vor weltliche oberkeiten, keyserliche majestät und andere

**Bid (English).** Pray ye also for the temporal magistrates, the Imperial Majesty and other

graciously keep therein them that are enlightened by God’s word, but give his grace unto them
that are not yet enlightened, that they may suffer and receive God’s word in their lands; but

**Bid (German).** Sonderlich danket Gott, das er unsere gnedige landsfürsten und herrn also

**Bid (English).** Especially give thanks to God that he hath so graced our gracious princes and
lords of the land that they love God’s word and let it be preached and further it in their land;
and pray that the Almighty would graciously keep them therein and vouchsafe him godly long life,
health and grace, that they may so lead their government that it may redound to the honour of
God and to the good of their princely Graces and their subjects, and that we may lead a quiet,
peaceable life under them in all godliness.

### J.4 Civil authority

*Witnesses:* Lüneburg 1564, Braunschweig-Wolfenbüttel 1569 and Oldenburg 1573.

**Bid (German).** Bittet auch vor unsere gnedige landsfürstin, das sie der allmechtig auch in

**Bid (English).** Pray ye also for our gracious princess of the land, that the Almighty would
have her also in his gracious protection, guard her from all mischief, and bless her in body and
soul. Pray ye also for the counsellors, officers and governors, and also the council of this
town, that God would give them grace to counsel well and faithfully to preside over and perform
the offices and charges committed unto them.

### J.5 The afflicted

*Witnesses:* Lüneburg 1564, Braunschweig-Wolfenbüttel 1569 and Oldenburg 1573.

**Bid (German).** Bittet auch vor alle angefochten und betrübte herzen, vor alle kranken,
schwangere frauen, junge kinder und alle, so in nöten sein, das der allmechtige wolle
dieselbigen alle trösten, behüten und ihnen in ihren nöten beystehen und zu hülf komen.

**Bid (English).** Pray ye also for all tempted and sorrowful hearts, for all the sick, women

them all, and stand by them and come to their help in their needs.

### J.6 Deliverance from calamities

*Witnesses:* Lüneburg 1564, Braunschweig-Wolfenbüttel 1569 and Oldenburg 1573.

## 15. Family K: Three collects from the pulpit

After an exhortation the minister reads three collects from the pulpit: thanksgiving or the
Church, the magistrates, and a special need, chosen from a bank.

- **Earliest witness (archetype):** Worms 1560.
- **Tradition:** Lutheran.
- **Language:** German.
- **Form:** exhortation + three collects.
- **Place in the service (Worms 1560):** On Sundays and feasts, when the choir has finished the
  first psalm or hymn, the minister goes into the pulpit, exhorts the people to prayer and reads
  three collects: (1) a thanksgiving for God’s gifts or a prayer for the preservation of the
  Church and God’s word (on feasts, a thanksgiving for the feast), (2) for the magistrates, (3) a
  special prayer as the time requires (peace, protection, fruits of the earth, rain …).

| Year | Witness | Territory | Sehling | Form |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1560 | Worms, Agendbüchlein 1560 | Worms (Imperial city) | Sehling 19, III/4, pp. 178–183 | exhortation + three collects |

Worms reads three collects from the pulpit: a thanksgiving, or a prayer for the Church and God's
word; one for the magistrates; and a third that varies with the time, chosen from the collect
bank that follows in the order. K.2 and K.3 are the two texts for the first collect, K.4 is the
collect for the magistrates, and K.5 is the rubric for the third.

**Sequence:** K.1 Exhortation / preface > K.2 Thanksgiving > K.3 The Church > K.4 Civil
authority > K.5 Special intercessions.

### K.1 Exhortation / preface

**Rubric (German).** VI. Vom gemeinen Gebett. Wiewol zu ende aller Predigten das Volck ernstlich
und vleissig für alle noth und anligen der gantzen Christenheit im Geistlichen, Weltlichen und
Haußregiment zubitten, Auch zur danckbarkeit gegen Gott für seine grosse und manigfältige
wolthaten genugsam ermahnet wirdt, Jedoch so werden umb mehrer erinnerung willen auch etliche
andere gemeine Gebett und Collecten gehalten und dem Volck deutlich fürgelesen inn volgender
ordnung. Am Sontag und Feiertagen, wann der Chor den ersten Psalm oder Geistlichen Gesang
vollendet hat, so gehet der Kirchendiener auff die Cantzel und ermahnet das Volck zum Gebett und
liset ihnen je auff einmal drey Collecten für. Die erste ist entzweder ein gemeine dancksagung
für allerley Gottes gaben oder ein gebett umb erhaltung der Kirchen und Göttlichs Worts oder, so
es an sonderlichen Festen ist, eine dancksagung für dieselbigen sonderen gaben, darvon man auff
dasselbige Fest pfleget zu handeln. Die ander von Weltlicher Oberkeit. Die dritt ist Specialis
und nach gelegenheit der zeit, eintzweder umb fried oder umb schutz oder für die früchte der
Erden oder für regen etc. und was dergleichen für sonderbare noth und anligen mehr fürfallen,
davon man die Christliche Kirch erinnern muß, auß nachgehender verzeichnuß.

**Rubric (English).** VI. Of the common prayer. Although at the end of all sermons the people
are sufficiently exhorted to pray earnestly and diligently for all the need and concerns of the
whole of Christendom in the spiritual, temporal and household government, and also to
thankfulness toward God for his great and manifold benefits, yet for the sake of greater
remembrance certain other common prayers and collects are also held and read plainly to the
people in the following order. On Sundays and feast days, when the choir hath finished the first
psalm or spiritual song, the minister goeth into the pulpit and exhorteth the people to prayer,
and readeth to them three collects at a time. The first is either a common thanksgiving for all
manner of God’s gifts, or a prayer for the preservation of the Church and God’s word, or, if it
be on special feasts, a thanksgiving for those special gifts whereof it is wont to be treated on
that feast. The second is of the temporal magistrates. The third is special, according to the
occasion of the time: either for peace, or for protection, or for the fruits of the earth, or
for rain, etc., and whatsoever like special need and concern may befall, whereof the Christian
Church must be put in mind, out of the register following.

**Rubric (English).** For the preservation and fruitful increase of God’s word.

**Prayer (English).** Almighty God, we beseech thee, give unto thy congregation thy Spirit and

### K.4 Civil authority

**Rubric (German).** Für Weltliche Obrigkeit allein.

**Rubric (English).** For the temporal magistrates alone.

**Prayer (German).** O Barmhertziger Himlischer Vatter, inn welches handt bestehet aller

**Prayer (English).** O merciful heavenly Father, in whose hand standeth all power of men and

## 17. Family M: Pulpit biddings in the Prone pattern

Early East Frisian pulpit biddings after the medieval Prone: three biddings, then Decalogue,
confession, Creed and Our Father.

- **Earliest witness (archetype):** Norden 1528.
- **Tradition:** Reformed
  (an early East Frisian evangelical order, printed by Sehling among the Reformed orders of East Frisia).
- **Language:** Low Dutch.
- **Form:** biddings, then Decalogue, confession, Creed, Our Father.
- **Place in the service (Norden 1528):** Every Sunday after the sermon (“na dat sermoen”):
  three biddings, then the Ten Commandments, a confession of sins, the Creed with a confession
  of faith, and the Our Father.

| Year | Witness | Territory | Sehling | Form |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1528 | Norden (East Frisia), Gottesdienstordnung [ca. 1528] (Reformed) | Norden, County of East Frisia | Sehling 7/1, IV/Ostfriesland Nr. 6, p. 431 | biddings + Decalogue + confession + Creed + Lord’s Prayer |

**Sequence:** M.1 Civil authority > M.2 Ministers of the Word > M.3 Enemies > M.4 Ten
Commandments.

### M.1 Civil authority

**Rubric (Low Dutch).** Dese nabeschrevene manier wert geholden to Norden alle Sondaechs na dat
sermoen.

**Rubric (English).** This manner written hereafter is observed at Norden every Sunday after the
sermon.

**Bid (Low Dutch).** I. Laet ons aenroepen en bidden God, onsen hemelsken Vader, voor onse
gnädige here mit sinen rade en amptluyden en voor alle overheit der werelt, dat hoer God regere
en regeren laet na sine woerde en Geeste tot gemeinen welvaren en vrede.

**Bid (English).** I. Let us call upon and pray God, our heavenly Father, for our gracious lord
with his council and officers, and for all the magistracy of the world, that God would govern
them and let them govern according to his word and Spirit, unto the common welfare and peace.

### M.2 Ministers of the Word

**Bid (Low Dutch).** II. Vor alle getrouwe dienaren en herderen der scapen Christi, dat se
vrymoedeliken en eendrachteliken Gods woert voeren en den gekruysten Christum verkondigen.

**Bid (English).** II. For all faithful ministers and shepherds of the sheep of Christ, that
they may set forth God’s word boldly and with one accord, and preach Christ crucified.

### M.3 Enemies

**Bid (Low Dutch).** III. Item voer alle onse vyanden en alle ongelovigen: heiden, Joden, Turken
en valsche Christen, dat se God met sinen woerde en Geeste verlichte en hem goede herderen geve.
Laet ons onse krancheit en sonden bekennen, die ons ut Gods geset en geboden werden openbaert.

**Bid (English).** III. Item, for all our enemies and all unbelievers: heathen, Jews, Turks and

shepherds. Let us confess our weakness and sins, which are made manifest to us out of God’s law

### M.4 Ten Commandments

**Rubric (Low Dutch).** Die tein geboden. … Ic gelove an God den Vader … Onse Vader in die
hemelen …

**Rubric (English).** The Ten Commandments. … I believe in God the Father … Our Father in the
heavens …

## 18. Family R1: Heidelberg 1563 prayer

The Reformed Sunday prayer after the sermon: confession and absolution, a long continuous prayer
(fruit of the Word, ministers, magistrates, all men, the persecuted, the afflicted, the
congregation), then the Our Father. Copied in Moers, Bentheim-Tecklenburg, Ysenburg-Birstein
1598 and, abridged, Lutheran Schaumburg 1614.

- **Earliest witness (archetype):** Kurpfalz 1563 (Heidelberg).
- **Tradition:** Reformed (Schaumburg 1614 Lutheran).
- **Language:** German.
- **Form:** confession + absolution + continuous prayer + Our Father.
- **Place in the service (Kurpfalz 1563):** Sunday morning after the sermon: exhortation to
  confession, confession, absolution (with retention), then the prayer and the Our Father; a psalm
  and the Aaronic blessing follow. The prayer before the sermon is a separate confession of sins.

### R1.1 Confession of sins

*Witnesses:* Kurpfalz 1563, Ysenburg-Birstein 1598 and Schaumburg 1614.

**Rubric (German).** Am Sontag nach der morgenpredig soll der kirchendiener sprechen:

**Rubric (English).** On Sunday after the morning sermon the minister shall say:

**Bid (German).** Ir geliebten in dem herrn, Dieweil wir in den geboten Gottes gleich als in
einem spiegel sehen, wie groß und vielfältig unser sünden sind, durch welche wir zeitliche und
ewige straf verdienen, so laßt uns diesselbige von hertzen unserm getreuen vater bekennen,
sprecht derhalben mit mir also:

**Bid (English).** Dearly beloved in the Lord, forasmuch as we see in the commandments of God as
in a glass how great and manifold our sins are, whereby we deserve temporal and eternal
punishment, let us confess the same from the heart unto our faithful Father; say therefore with
me thus:

**Prayer (German).** Ich armer sünder bekenn für dir, meinem Gott und schöpfer, daß ich leider
schwerlich und mannigfältig wider dich gesündiget hab, nicht allein mit eusserlichen groben
sünden, sonder vilmehr mit innerlicher angeborner blindheyt, unglauben, zweifelung,
kleinmütigkeyt, ungedult, hoffart, bösem geitz, heimlichen neid, haß und mißvergunst, auch
andern bösen dücken, wie du, mein herr und Gott, an mir erkennest und ich leider nit gnugsam
erkennen kan, die reuen mich und sind mir leid und beger von hertzen gnad, durch deinen lieben
son Jesum Christum.

**Prayer (English).** I, poor sinner, confess before thee, my God and Creator, that I, alas,
have sinned grievously and manifoldly against thee, not only with outward gross sins, but much
more with inward, inborn blindness, unbelief, doubting, faintheartedness, impatience, pride,
evil covetousness, secret envy, hatred and grudging, and other evil wiles, as thou, my Lord and
God, knowest in me, and I, alas, cannot sufficiently know; they repent me and grieve me, and I
desire grace from my heart, through thy dear Son Jesus Christ.

**Prayer (English).** Hear now the sure comfort of the grace of God, which he promiseth in his

## 21. Family R4: à Lasco/Micron (London stranger church)

The London Dutch stranger church's prayer after the sermon (German version for East Frisia),
still praying for the King of England and the city of London.

- **Earliest witness (archetype):** London 1554 (à Lasco/Micron).
- **Tradition:** Reformed.
- **Language:** German.
- **Form:** continuous prayer + Our Father.
- **Place in the service (London 1554 (Micron)):** Sunday morning after the sermon, the prayer
  for the fruit of the Word, the Decalogue, confession, declaration of loosing and binding, and
  the Creed; then the Our Father, and baptism, the Supper or marriage; a psalm and the blessing
  with a charge to remember the poor. “Das grosse gebet vor die gemeine notdurft” is said after
  every sermon.

### R4.1 Thanksgiving

**Rubric (German).** Nach diser bekäntnuß fahet der diener das gemeine gebet an für allerley not
der christlichen gemeine auf dise weise:

**Rubric (English).** After this confession the minister beginneth the common prayer for all
manner of need of the Christian congregation after this manner:

**Prayer (German).** O almechtiger, himlischer und barmherziger Vater, nachdem es dir gefallen
hat, durch deine unentliche barmherzigkeit uns auß der finsternuß der unwissenheit und der
römischen abgötterey zu erlösen und uns under andern die geheimnuß des heiligen evangelions
deines lieben Sons zu offenbaren, darfür wir dir von ganzen herzen danken, so bitten wir dich

**Prayer (English).** O almighty, heavenly and merciful Father, forasmuch as it hath pleased
thee of thine infinite mercy to deliver us out of the darkness of ignorance and of the Roman

### R4.2 The Church

**Prayer (German).** Wir bitten dich auch, allerheiligster Vater, für die gemeine kirch deines
lieben Sohns Jesu Christi, die durch die ganze weite welt zerstreuet ist, in welcher die
gottselige lere deines Sohns (nachdem der greuel und abgötterey des römischen antichrists
außgerottet ist) geleret und erhalten wird. Wehre von denselbigen alle falsche hirten und lerer
und sende gottselige, getreue und ernsthaftige arbeiter in deine ernd, welche nit ihre eigene
ehr suchen, sonder allein deine ehre und die erbauung deiner gemeine.

**Prayer (English).** We pray thee also, most holy Father, for the common Church of thy dear Son
Jesus Christ, which is scattered throughout the whole wide world, wherein the godly doctrine of
thy Son is taught and maintained (the abomination and idolatry of the Roman Antichrist being
rooted out). Keep from her all false shepherds and teachers, and send godly, faithful and
earnest labourers into thine harvest, which seek not their own honour, but thine honour only and
the edifying of thy congregation.

### R4.3 Civil authority

**Prayer (German).** Und erstlich bitten wir, allergnedigister Vater, für die gemeine dieses
reichs Engeland und für alle seine diener und insonderheit für unsern gnedigisten herren könig
N. N., wollest ihn behüten und bewaren und mit deinem Geist regieren, auf daß er sein volk unter
unserm gemeinem haubt Jesu Christo regieren möge, daß wir ein gerüigs und stilles leben under
ihm in aller gottseligkeit und erbarkeit nach deinem wort durch deine gnade füren mögen. Wir
bitten auch für der königlichen majestät hoffgesind und den adel, auch für alle herren und
oberkeit des ganzen königreichs und vörnemlich für den wollweisen hohen rat königlicher
majestet, begabe ihn mit den geist des rats, der sterke und bestendigkeit. Gebe ihnen auch den
geist der einigkeit und des friedes, auf daß sie allem dem, das recht und gut ist, in ihrer
regierung folgen und also eine christliche ruhe und frieden in disem reich fürdern und erhalten
helfen.

**Prayer (English).** And first we pray, most gracious Father, for the congregation of this
realm of England and for all its ministers, and especially for our most gracious lord King N.
N.: preserve and keep him and govern him with thy Spirit, that he may rule his people under our
common Head Jesus Christ, that we may lead under him a quiet and still life in all godliness and
honesty according to thy word by thy grace. We pray also for the King’s Majesty’s household and
the nobility, and for all lords and magistrates of the whole kingdom, and chiefly for the right
wise high council of the King’s Majesty: endue it with the spirit of counsel, of strength and of
constancy. Give them also the spirit of unity and of peace, that in their government they may
follow all that is right and good, and so help to further and maintain a Christian quiet and
peace in this realm.

### R4.4 The present congregation

**Prayer (German).** Wollest auch das ganze volk dises reichs mit deinem Geist begaben, daß es
die stimme deiner propheten willig höre und der lehre deines Sohns Jesu Christi wahr nemme und
in derselbigen täglich mehr und mehr zunehme, auch daß es in dem waren gehorsam (denn es der
königlichen majestät und dero verordneten schuldig ist) bestendig bleibe, zur wolfart dises
reichs und erbauung der christlichen gemeine. Wir bitten weiter deine göttliche gnade für diese

derselbigen mit deinem Geist, daß sie in deiner forcht treulich und weißlich ihren dienst
versehen möge.

**Prayer (English).** Endue also the whole people of this realm with thy Spirit, that it may
willingly hear the voice of thy prophets, and take heed to the doctrine of thy Son Jesus Christ,
and increase therein more and more from day to day; and that it may abide constant in the true
obedience which it oweth to the King’s Majesty and his officers, to the welfare of this realm
and the edifying of the Christian congregation. We pray further thy divine grace for this city
of London, that thou wouldest turn away from it all manner of plagues (which we daily deserve),
and preserve it in all godliness, peace and common quiet. Govern also the magistrates thereof
with thy Spirit, that they may perform their office faithfully and wisely in thy fear.

### R4.5 The present congregation

**Prayer (German).** Insonderheit bitten wir dich, o allergütigster Vater, fur dise unsere
frembde gemeine, du wollest deine gunst gegen ihr also, wie du sie durch deine wunderbarliche
gnade hieher gepflanzet, hinfur beweisen, daß sie von aller weltlicher tyranney und falschen
lere durch deine macht und barmherzigkeit geschützet, täglich je mehr und mehr in aller
gottseligkeit und warer einigkeit der herzen zunemen möge.

**Prayer (English).** Especially we pray thee, O most gracious Father, for this our stranger
congregation, that thou wouldest henceforth so show thy favour toward her, as thou hast planted
her here by thy wonderful grace, that, being defended by thy power and mercy from all worldly
tyranny and false doctrine, she may increase daily more and more in all godliness and true unity
of hearts.

### R4.6 The erring & unbelievers

**Prayer (German).** Wir bitten dich auch demütiglich für alle königen, fürsten, oberkeiten und
völker, so die stimme deines Sohns (dieweil sie mit der antichristischen abgötterey verblendet
sind) noch nit haben erkennen können und die lebendige glieder deines Sohns (den sie nit
erkennen) feindlich durch unwissenheit verfolgen, du wollest sie mit allen andern verblenten
oder (durch irgenteine schwachheit) verfüreten personen zu dem warhaftigen liecht und rechten
wege bringen, auf daß wir alle in einen schaffstal under dem einigen hirten unser seelen Christo
Jesu versamlet, dich einmütiglich preisen in ewigkeit.

**Prayer (English).** We pray thee also humbly for all kings, princes, magistrates and peoples
which (being blinded with the antichristian idolatry) have not yet been able to know the voice
of thy Son, and through ignorance persecute as enemies the living members of thy Son (whom they
know not): that thou wouldest bring them, with all other persons blinded or (through any
weakness) led astray, to the true light and the right way, that we all, gathered into one
sheepfold under the one Shepherd of our souls, Christ Jesus, may praise thee with one mind for
ever.

### R4.7 The persecuted

**Prayer (German).** Zum letzten bitten wir auch für alle unsere mitbrüder, uber den ganzen
erdkreiß zerstreuet, welche umb der rechten bekantnuß willen deines Sons und seiner lere durch
den antichristen under dem kreuz underdrucket sind, und insonderheit wollest trösten mit deinem
heiligen Geist N. N. und ihre herzen mit deiner göttlichen kraft in dem rechten glauben von oben
herab also sterken, daß sie alles, so du ihnen zusendest, geduldiglich, menlich und mit
danksagung tragen mögen; und daß sie ohne furcht bestendig deinen und deines Sohns namen für den
menschen herrlich bekennen und groß machen, es sey durch leben oder sterben, wie du das zu
deiner ehren erkennest dienstlich sein.

**Prayer (English).** Lastly we pray also for all our brethren scattered over the whole earth,
which for the right confession of thy Son and his doctrine are oppressed under the cross by
Antichrist; and especially wilt thou comfort with thy Holy Spirit N. N., and so strengthen their
hearts from above with thy divine power in the right faith, that they may bear all that thou
sendest them patiently, manfully and with thanksgiving; and that without fear they may
steadfastly confess gloriously before men thy name and the name of thy Son and make it great, be
it by life or by death, as thou knowest it to serve to thine honour.

### R4.8 The afflicted

**Prayer (German).** Sonderlich bitten wir auch für alle brüder, die du mit krankheit, armut,
gefängnuß, verbannung oder anderm elend des geistes oder leibs billich versuchet und geübet
hast, daß du sie in ihrer not nicht verlassen, sonder ihr kreuz nach deiner väterlichen
barmherzigkeit linderen wollest oder ihnen sterke und gedult geben, daß sie bestendig tragen
alles, was du ihnen nach deinem wolgefallen zusendest, auf daß sie auß ihrer straffe verstehen
mögen, daß sie von dir, als ihrem barmherzigen, lieben Vater, probieret werden zur bewärung
ihres glaubens und also sich zu dir bekeren.

**Prayer (English).** Especially we pray also for all the brethren whom thou hast justly tried
and exercised with sickness, poverty, imprisonment, banishment or other misery of spirit or
body, that thou wouldest not forsake them in their need, but mitigate their cross according to
thy fatherly mercy, or give them strength and patience to bear steadfastly all that thou sendest
them according to thy good pleasure; that they may understand by their chastisement that they
are proved of thee, as their merciful, dear Father, for the trial of their faith, and so be
converted unto thee.

### R4.9 Lord's Prayer

**Prayer (German).** Alle dise ding begeren wir von dir, allergnedigister Vater, durch deine
väterliche gnad und gunst, die du in deinem Sohne gegen uns bewiesen hast. Und in diser
ungezweifelten hoffnung ruffen wir demütiglich deinen namen ahn durch denselbigen deinen
geliebten Sohn Jesum Christum, wie wir von ihm gelehret sind, sprechende: Unser Vater, der du
bist in den himlen etc.

**Prayer (English).** All these things we desire of thee, most gracious Father, through thy
fatherly grace and favour which thou hast shown toward us in thy Son. And in this undoubted hope
we humbly call upon thy name through the same thy beloved Son Jesus Christ, as we are taught of
him, saying: Our Father, which art in the heavens, etc.

gemeine.

**Rubric (English).** Here it is to be noted that other prayers are oftentimes added here before
